What Is Wix?

Wix is one of the world's most popular website builders, powering well over 200 million registered users' sites. It's a fully hosted platform: design, hosting, security, and updates are all handled for you, so there's nothing to install or maintain — the opposite of the WordPress model, where flexibility comes with upkeep.

Over the past few years Wix has evolved from "easy homepage maker" into a genuine business platform, adding Wix Stores for ecommerce, Wix Bookings for appointments, built-in email marketing, and an AI website builder that assembles a working site from a short conversation about your business. For agencies and designers there's also Wix Studio, a more powerful professional environment on the same infrastructure.

Key Features

Drag-and-drop editor with real design freedom

Wix's classic editor is the most flexible in the builder category: you can place any element anywhere on the page, layer sections, and fine-tune spacing without being locked to a rigid grid. Hundreds of designer templates cover nearly every industry, and each is fully customizable. The catch — and it's a famous one — is that you can't switch templates after publishing without rebuilding your content.

AI website builder

Describe your business in a short chat and Wix's AI generates a complete site — structure, copy, images, and a matching theme — that you then refine in the editor. It's a first draft, not a finished product, but it turns the blank-page problem into a 10-minute editing session. AI text and image tools inside the editor help with ongoing tweaks.

Ecommerce and bookings

From the Core plan up, you can sell products, manage inventory, accept payments, and recover abandoned carts; the Business plan adds advanced ecommerce features like multiple currencies and automated sales tax. Wix Bookings — scheduling, staff calendars, and paid sessions — is a standout for service businesses, and it's included rather than a paid add-on.

SEO and marketing tools

Wix's old reputation for weak SEO is outdated: you get full control of meta tags, structured data, redirects, and sitemaps, plus built-in email campaigns and social tools. For most small-business sites, nothing in the SEO toolbox is missing anymore.

App Market

500+ apps extend the platform — chat widgets, reviews, events, restaurant menus, and more. Many are free to start; the good ones typically run ~$3–$20/month, which is worth factoring into your real monthly cost.

Who Should Use Wix?

  • Local service businesses — salons, trainers, clinics — that live on Wix Bookings and a professional-looking site.
  • Creators and freelancers who want a portfolio that doesn't look like a template, without hiring a designer.
  • Small shops selling a modest catalog where builder-grade ecommerce is plenty.
  • Anyone starting from zero — the AI builder plus free plan is the lowest-friction on-ramp in the category.

If you're building a serious online store first and a website second, start with a commerce-native platform instead — see our Shopify review for why checkout and app depth matter more than page design at scale.

Top Alternatives

  • Squarespace — better if you want stricter design guardrails and polished templates you can't accidentally break.
  • Shopify — better for ecommerce-first businesses; its checkout, payments, and app ecosystem are in another league for selling.
  • WordPress.com — better for content-heavy sites and blogs where portability and plugins outweigh drag-and-drop ease.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Wix really free?

There's a genuinely free plan, but it uses a Wix subdomain (yoursite.wixsite.com) and shows Wix ads, so it's best treated as an unlimited trial. For a business site you'll want at least the Light plan (~$17/mo) for a custom domain and ad removal.

Can I switch my Wix template later?

No — once your site is published, you can restyle nearly everything, but you can't swap to a different template without rebuilding pages. Spend real time choosing your starting template, or let the AI builder generate the structure and customize from there.

Is Wix good for SEO?

Yes, for the vast majority of small-business sites. You control titles, meta descriptions, structured data, redirects, and sitemaps, and Wix sites can and do rank well. Enormous content sites with complex technical SEO needs are the main case where a self-hosted platform still wins.

Wix vs Shopify — which should I choose for a store?

Wix if the store is one part of a broader business site and your catalog is modest. Shopify if selling is the business — its checkout conversion, payment options, and fulfillment apps outclass any general-purpose builder. Our Shopify review breaks down the details.

Final Verdict

Wix earns a 4.5/5. No mainstream builder makes it easier to get a genuinely professional-looking site live, and the AI builder plus Bookings plus Stores combination covers what most small businesses actually need in 2026. We dock half a point for the template lock-in, the lack of a clean exit path, and add-on costs that quietly raise the real monthly bill. If you know those trade-offs going in, Wix is an easy tool to recommend — and hard to leave, in both senses.
