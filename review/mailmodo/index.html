What Is Mailmodo?

Mailmodo is an email marketing platform built around one big idea: email shouldn't just be something people read — it should be something they can act on. Using Google's AMP for Email technology, Mailmodo lets you embed working forms, surveys, quizzes, calendars, spin-the-wheel games, and even shopping carts directly inside the email body. Subscribers respond right there in Gmail or another supporting client, with no "click here to open a landing page" detour.

That matters because every redirect leaks conversions. In a typical funnel, a chunk of readers who click through never complete the form on the other side. Mailmodo's pitch is that collapsing the funnel into the inbox recovers those losses — and case studies routinely report 2–3x higher submission rates on in-email forms versus linked landing pages. Alongside the AMP headline act, Mailmodo covers the standard email marketing bases: campaigns, customer journeys, segmentation, a transactional email API, and analytics.

Key Features

Interactive AMP emails

This is the reason Mailmodo exists. The template gallery includes ready-made interactive blocks — NPS surveys, multi-step forms, product carousels, quizzes, appointment calendars, and cart widgets — that you drop into an email like any other content block. Responses flow back into Mailmodo (or out to your stack via webhooks) in real time. For clients that don't support AMP, such as Apple Mail, Mailmodo automatically sends a standard HTML fallback with a linked form, so nobody gets a broken email.

No-code template builder

The drag-and-drop editor is genuinely no-code: interactivity that would normally require an AMP developer is configured with dropdowns and toggles. There's a large library of prebuilt templates organized by use case (onboarding, feedback, ecommerce, webinars), and the editor previews both the AMP version and the HTML fallback so you can sanity-check each before sending.

Customer journeys & automation

Mailmodo's visual journey builder handles the automation staples — welcome series, drip sequences, cart abandonment, re-engagement — with triggers based on list entry, events, form submissions, or API calls. It's capable and easy to learn, though power users coming from ActiveCampaign will notice fewer branching conditions and no built-in sales pipeline. For most marketing-led teams, the depth is sufficient.

Transactional email API

A quietly excellent feature: the same platform sends your receipts, password resets, and notification emails via API or SMTP — and those transactional emails can be interactive too. A feedback survey embedded in an order confirmation is exactly the kind of high-open-rate placement most tools can't touch.

Deliverability tooling

Mailmodo takes deliverability seriously, with managed IP warmup, domain authentication guidance (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), and send-time optimization. In our test sends, inbox placement was consistently strong. Since interactive emails are worthless in a spam folder, this focus is more than box-ticking.

Who Should Use Mailmodo?

  • SaaS teams collecting onboarding feedback, NPS scores, and feature votes without begging users to click through to a survey tool.
  • Ecommerce stores that want in-email carts, product quizzes, and spin-to-win offers to squeeze more revenue from the same list.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Mailmodo have a free plan?

No. Mailmodo offers a generous 21-day free trial with no credit card required, but there is no permanently free tier. If a free plan is essential, Mailchimp's free tier is the usual starting point.

Do AMP emails work in every inbox?

No — AMP is supported in Gmail, Yahoo Mail, and a few other clients, but not Apple Mail or Outlook desktop. Mailmodo handles this by automatically sending an HTML fallback version with a linked form, so every subscriber gets a working email either way.

Why does Mailmodo need sender approval for AMP emails?

Google and other mail clients require senders to be whitelisted before AMP emails render for recipients. Mailmodo guides you through the registration process, which typically takes a few days and requires a healthy sending reputation. Until approval, your subscribers simply receive the HTML fallback.

Mailmodo vs ActiveCampaign — which should I pick?

Pick Mailmodo if in-email conversions (forms, surveys, bookings, carts) are your priority. Pick ActiveCampaign if you need deeper multi-step automation and an integrated sales CRM. Our head-to-head comparison breaks it down in detail.

Final Verdict

Mailmodo earns its rating of 4.4/5. It does one thing better than anyone in its price range — turning emails from static messages into working micro-apps — and backs it up with solid automation, a real transactional API, and strong deliverability. It's not the tool for CRM-heavy sales teams or bargain-hunting newsletter senders, but if conversions happen in your subscribers' inboxes, Mailmodo is the most interesting email platform we've tested in 2026 — and the 21-day trial gives you plenty of runway to prove the lift on your own list.
