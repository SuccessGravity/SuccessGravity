What Is AffiliateWP?

AffiliateWP is a self-hosted affiliate management plugin for WordPress, originally launched in 2014 by Pippin Williamson. The plugin is now developed and maintained by Awesome Motive — the same team behind WPForms, MonsterInsights, and WPBeginner — bringing enterprise-grade support and a development cadence to what started as an indie product.

The core proposition is straightforward: instead of paying a monthly SaaS fee that scales with your affiliate count or revenue, you pay a flat annual license fee and run everything on your own WordPress install. Your affiliate data, payouts, and creatives all live on your servers, which matters for businesses with strict data-residency requirements or those wary of third-party data exposure.

AffiliateWP integrates natively with the most popular WordPress commerce and membership plugins — WooCommerce, Easy Digital Downloads, MemberPress, Gravity Forms, Formidable Forms, WP Simple Pay, and others — so the affiliate layer slots into an existing stack rather than requiring a platform rebuild.

Key Features

Unlimited affiliates and creatives

All plans support an unlimited number of affiliates and an unlimited number of promotional creatives (banners, text links, coupons). There is no per-affiliate pricing tier that penalizes growth, which is a meaningful advantage over some SaaS competitors that charge more as the network scales.

Real-time referral tracking

AffiliateWP records referrals as they happen, with a live dashboard showing pending, approved, and paid commissions. Affiliates log into a dedicated portal to view their earnings, grab their links, and download their creatives without needing to contact the program manager.

WooCommerce and EDD integration

The WooCommerce integration is the strongest in its class among WordPress-native tools. It handles product-specific commission rates, coupon-based referral tracking (so an affiliate's coupon code also credits them for the sale), and recurring commissions for WooCommerce Subscriptions. Easy Digital Downloads support is equally deep, covering software license renewals and variable pricing.

Pay-per-click and coupon tracking

Beyond the standard pay-per-sale model, AffiliateWP supports pay-per-click tracking and coupon-code-based referrals. Affiliates can be credited when visitors click their links even if they don't immediately convert, and coupon codes can be tied to individual affiliates for influencer and social media campaigns where traditional link tracking is harder.

REST API and developer tools

A full REST API lets developers pull affiliate data into custom dashboards, sync with payroll systems, or build integrations with tools AffiliateWP doesn't natively support. The plugin also fires standard WordPress action hooks throughout the referral lifecycle, making it extensible for custom workflows.

Payouts Service

AffiliateWP's optional Payouts Service (available on Professional and above) lets you pay affiliates directly through the plugin dashboard via bank transfer, without manually exporting CSVs to PayPal or Stripe. According to AffiliateWP's documentation, the service uses Stripe Connect under the hood — verify current availability and fees on affiliatewp.com.

Pros and Cons

  • Annual flat fee — no per-affiliate or per-sale charges
  • Self-hosted: full data ownership on your server
  • Unlimited affiliates on all plans

Who Should Use AffiliateWP?

  • WooCommerce store owners running a physical or digital product business who want deep order-level commission tracking without leaving WordPress.
  • Membership site operators on MemberPress, Restrict Content Pro, or similar plugins who need recurring commission support for subscriptions.
  • Digital product creators using Easy Digital Downloads who want to recruit an affiliate network around software licenses or digital downloads.
  • Agencies managing affiliate programs for multiple WordPress client sites — the Professional and Ultimate plans cover unlimited installs.

If your business runs on Shopify, a custom SaaS stack, or anything outside WordPress, AffiliateWP is not the right tool. For those use cases, a hosted solution like Rewardful or Tapfiliate is a better fit.

Top Alternatives

  • Rewardful — the clearest alternative for SaaS companies: hosted, Stripe-native, and live in minutes without touching a server.
  • Tapfiliate — a hosted affiliate platform with broader payment processor support and a visual campaign builder; starts around $59/mo.
  • Post Affiliate Pro — a more enterprise-grade hosted solution with multi-tier commissions and advanced fraud detection; starting around $97/mo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AffiliateWP work with WooCommerce?

Yes. AffiliateWP integrates natively with WooCommerce, automatically tracking sales and commissions when affiliates refer customers to your store. The integration is included at all pricing tiers with no extra add-on required.

Is AffiliateWP a one-time payment?

AffiliateWP uses annual licensing, not a one-time lifetime fee. Personal, Plus, Professional, and Ultimate plans are billed yearly. Pricing is per-site (or multi-site at higher tiers), not per-affiliate, which makes it cost-effective as your affiliate program grows.

Can AffiliateWP handle recurring commissions for subscriptions?

Yes. AffiliateWP supports recurring referrals for subscription products through integrations with WooCommerce Subscriptions, MemberPress, and similar plugins. Each renewal payment can trigger an affiliate commission automatically.

What is the difference between AffiliateWP and Rewardful?

AffiliateWP is a self-hosted WordPress plugin — your data stays on your server and there is no per-affiliate or per-sale SaaS fee. Rewardful is a hosted SaaS tool that plugs directly into Stripe or Paddle. If you run a WordPress site, AffiliateWP is usually the lower total cost of ownership; if you run a SaaS business billed through Stripe, Rewardful is faster to set up.

Final Verdict

AffiliateWP earns a rating of 4.5/5. For WordPress businesses — particularly WooCommerce stores, digital download shops, and membership sites — it is the most capable and cost-effective affiliate management solution available. The flat annual license, unlimited affiliates, and deep native integrations give it a total-cost-of-ownership advantage over monthly SaaS tools at comparable feature depth. The UI could be more modern, and non-WordPress businesses should look elsewhere, but for the audience it serves, AffiliateWP is difficult to beat.
