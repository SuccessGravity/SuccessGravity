What Is OpusClip?

OpusClip is an AI-powered video repurposing tool that takes long-form video content — webinars, podcasts, interviews, YouTube videos, Zoom recordings — and automatically generates short clips optimized for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn Video. It was founded in 2022 and has grown rapidly as short-form video consumption has accelerated across platforms.

The core mechanic is simple: upload a video URL or file, tell OpusClip the clip length you want (30, 60, or 90 seconds), and the AI identifies the strongest moments, trims them to length, adds animated captions, resizes the frame to 9:16 vertical format, and rates each clip with a "Virality Score" indicating its estimated potential to perform well on social media.

Key Features

AI clip selection and Virality Score

OpusClip's AI analyzes your video for hooks, storytelling beats, emotional peaks, and quotable moments. Each identified clip receives a Virality Score from 0–100, based on factors like hook strength, pace, and clarity of a core message. In practice, scores above 80 reliably surface the most watchable moments — though the AI still benefits from a quick human review before publishing.

Auto-captions with speaker tracking

Every generated clip includes animated word-by-word captions synced to the audio, with automatic speaker detection in multi-person videos. Caption styles are customizable — font, color, animation — and caption accuracy is strong across standard English accents. Non-English and accented speech quality varies.

Smart reframe (portrait crop)

When working with landscape (16:9) source footage, OpusClip's AI tracks the active speaker and automatically crops and zooms to keep them centered in the 9:16 frame. This feature alone saves significant manual work compared to resizing footage by hand in a traditional editor.

B-roll and AI video edits

On higher-tier plans, OpusClip can insert relevant stock footage or AI-generated B-roll segments between talking-head moments, add dynamic screen recordings or slides, and apply basic color grading.

Social scheduling and direct publishing

Paid plans include a built-in social scheduler. You can connect TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn accounts and schedule clips to post directly — eliminating the download-upload cycle that eats time in most content workflows.

Team workspaces

Business plans support multiple users, shared projects, and review workflows — useful for agencies managing content for multiple clients or social media teams with approval processes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does OpusClip have a free plan?

Yes. OpusClip's free plan includes 60 upload minutes and 1 hour of AI clipping credits per month. Downloads are watermarked on the free tier. No credit card is required to sign up.

How accurate is OpusClip's viral score?

The viral score is a useful shortlist signal. Clips rated 80+ consistently have strong openings and maintained energy. That said, the AI doesn't know your specific audience or platform algorithm history — treat high-scoring clips as priority candidates for human review, not auto-publish material.

Can OpusClip post directly to social media?

Yes. Paid plans include direct publishing to TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn Video, with a built-in scheduler. This removes the need to manually export and re-upload clips to each platform.

How does OpusClip compare to Pictory?

They serve different purposes. OpusClip extracts short clips from existing long-form video — ideal for podcasters and webinar hosts. Pictory creates new video content from text scripts or articles. If you're repurposing existing video, OpusClip wins. If you're creating new video from blog posts or scripts, Pictory is the better fit.
