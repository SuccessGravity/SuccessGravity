What Is ClickUp?

ClickUp is a cloud-based productivity and project management platform founded in 2017. Its core promise is consolidation: tasks, docs, whiteboards, chat, time tracking, goals, and automation — all in a single workspace, so teams can stop juggling five different SaaS tools.

Since its launch, ClickUp has grown to over 10 million users and is widely used by everything from freelancers to Fortune 500 teams. It has expanded rapidly: ClickUp AI was added in 2023, improved substantially in 2024, and by 2026 is a genuinely useful layer for drafting, summarizing, and automating within the platform.

The platform is organized around a clear hierarchy: Workspace → Spaces → Folders → Lists → Tasks. This gives teams a lot of structural flexibility, but also means there are real decisions to make upfront about how to organize your work — a setup investment that pays off for teams with diverse workflows.

Key Features

Multiple views for every workflow

ClickUp's views are one of its biggest differentiators. Every list of tasks can be viewed as a:

  • List — a traditional task list with subtasks and custom fields
  • Board — a Kanban view for visual workflow stages
  • Calendar — deadline-based scheduling
  • Gantt — dependency tracking and project timelines
  • Table — a spreadsheet-like view with custom fields
  • Timeline, Workload, Mind Map, Whiteboard — and more

Being able to switch views without changing the underlying data means different team members can work in the format that suits them, using the same tasks.

Custom fields and automation

ClickUp supports 35+ custom field types — text, number, dropdown, formula, relationship, currency, and more. Combined with automations (trigger → action rules), you can build workflows that rival purpose-built CRM or project tracking tools. Over 100 automation templates are available, and you can connect to Zapier or Make for additional integrations.

Docs

ClickUp Docs is a fully featured document editor that lives inside your workspace. You can create wikis, SOPs, project briefs, and meeting notes — and link them directly to tasks. It's not quite as polished as Notion's editor, but the deep integration with tasks and workflows is a major convenience for teams already living in ClickUp.

ClickUp AI (Brain)

ClickUp Brain is the platform's AI layer, available on paid plans. It can generate task descriptions, summarize lengthy Docs, draft emails, write project briefs, and answer questions about your workspace data ("what tasks are overdue in the Marketing space?"). By 2026, Brain has become capable enough to materially speed up repetitive writing and status-check work inside the platform.

Time tracking

Native time tracking is built in — you can log time against tasks, set estimates, and view reports. This is particularly useful for agencies billing by the hour or teams that track utilization. Integrations with tools like Toggl and Harvest exist if you need more advanced time reporting.

Goals and dashboards

ClickUp Goals link tasks and lists to measurable targets — OKRs, sprint velocity, revenue milestones. Dashboards pull data across the workspace into customizable charts and metric cards, giving managers a real-time snapshot without building a separate reporting layer.

Integrations

ClickUp integrates natively with 1,000+ tools, including Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Zoom, Figma, HubSpot, Salesforce, and the full Google Workspace. For teams that can't fully consolidate, ClickUp generally plays well with the surrounding stack.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ClickUp really free?

Yes. ClickUp's Free Forever plan supports unlimited members and unlimited tasks, with 100MB of storage and access to most core features. Automations, certain views, and ClickUp AI require a paid plan, but the free tier is genuinely useful for small teams.

ClickUp vs Asana — which is better?

Asana is simpler, cleaner, and easier to onboard. ClickUp has far more features and a more generous free plan but requires more setup. For teams that want speed-to-productivity, Asana wins. For teams that want maximum customization at a lower price, ClickUp wins.

Does ClickUp have a time tracking feature?

Yes. ClickUp includes native time tracking on all plans. You can log time manually or start a timer directly from a task, then view reports by member, date range, or task. For more advanced time reporting and billing, integrations with Toggl or Harvest are available.

Is ClickUp good for remote teams?

Yes. ClickUp is fully cloud-based and works across web, desktop, and mobile. Its real-time collaboration features — live doc editing, comments, mentions, whiteboards — are well-suited to async and remote work. The main friction is the mobile app, which is less polished than the desktop version.

What is ClickUp Brain?

ClickUp Brain is the platform's built-in AI tool. It can answer questions about your workspace, write and summarize Docs, draft task descriptions, create action items from meeting notes, and automate routine writing tasks. It's available as a paid add-on (~$5/user/mo) on paid plans.

Final Verdict

ClickUp earns a 4.5/5 in our 2026 review. The free plan alone makes it worth trying for any team: unlimited members, real tasks, and enough views to run a real workflow at $0. On paid plans, the value per dollar is extremely high compared to Asana ($10.99+/user) or Monday.com ($9+/user) — you get more features for less money.

The honest caveat is that ClickUp rewards teams that invest in it. The setup takes time, and the feature depth that makes it powerful can feel like chaos if you never configure it. But for teams willing to spend a few hours on onboarding — or use one of ClickUp's excellent template libraries to jump-start setup — it's one of the most capable productivity platforms available in 2026.
