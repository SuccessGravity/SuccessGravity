What Is Squarespace?

Squarespace is an all-in-one website builder and hosting platform founded in 2004. It lets anyone create a professional website without touching code — drag, drop, customize, publish. Over the years it has grown into a genuine small-business platform, adding ecommerce, scheduling (Acuity Scheduling), email marketing, and analytics alongside its core website builder.

What sets Squarespace apart from competitors like Wix or Weebly is its commitment to design quality. Every template is crafted by professional designers, and the editor enforces layout consistency so pages look intentional rather than thrown together. For visually driven industries — photographers, agencies, boutique retailers, restaurants — that built-in aesthetic standard is a major selling point.

Today, Squarespace powers millions of websites worldwide and has expanded from a pure site builder into a multi-product platform that can handle a significant chunk of a small business's online presence.

Key Features

Award-winning templates

Squarespace offers 160+ professionally designed templates organized by industry. Unlike some builders where templates are hit-or-miss, Squarespace's entire catalog is maintained to a high standard — clean layouts, solid typography, and mobile-responsive out of the box. Switching templates after launch is possible, though it requires some reformatting.

Drag-and-drop editor (Fluid Engine)

The newer Fluid Engine editor allows true freeform positioning — you can place elements precisely on a grid without being locked into column-based sections. It's more flexible than the old section editor, though still less freeform than Wix's pixel-perfect drag-and-drop. Most users find it intuitive after a short learning curve.

Built-in ecommerce

All plans include basic online store capabilities; the Commerce plans unlock advanced features. You can sell physical and digital products, subscriptions, courses, and services. Squarespace handles checkout, shipping labels, inventory, and abandoned-cart recovery. The checkout experience is clean and conversion-optimized, though transaction fees apply on lower plans (0.9%–2% depending on tier).

SEO tools

Squarespace includes solid built-in SEO: auto-generated sitemaps, clean URL structures, meta title/description editing, alt text, schema markup for products and events, SSL included, and a built-in keyword suggestion tool. It won't replace a dedicated tool like Semrush, but it handles the fundamentals without plugins.

Marketing suite

Squarespace Email Campaigns (included on higher tiers or as an add-on) lets you design and send email newsletters that match your site's style. It's not as powerful as ActiveCampaign for automation, but for simple newsletters to a site's audience it gets the job done without a third-party tool.

Squarespace AI

The platform has introduced AI tools for copy generation (page text, product descriptions, email subject lines) and a new AI site builder that generates a full page layout from a text prompt. In 2026 these features have matured enough to meaningfully speed up setup, though they work best as a starting draft rather than final copy.

Analytics and extensions

Built-in analytics show visits, page views, traffic sources, and commerce data. Squarespace Extensions add third-party integrations (ShipStation, QuickBooks, Printful, Mailchimp, and others), though the marketplace is smaller than the WordPress plugin ecosystem.

Who Should Use Squarespace?

  • Photographers and visual artists who need a portfolio that looks as good as their work — Squarespace's image-handling and gallery layouts are best in class.
  • Restaurants, salons, and local service businesses that want a professional site with booking (via Acuity integration), menus, and a polished first impression.
  • Coaches, consultants, and course creators who want to sell digital products, courses, or scheduling without cobbling together multiple tools.
  • Small boutique ecommerce stores (up to a few hundred SKUs) that prioritize brand aesthetics over a vast product catalog.
  • Anyone tired of WordPress maintenance — if you want a site that just works, without plugins, updates, and security patches, Squarespace's all-in-one model is a genuine relief.

If you need deep customization, hundreds of integrations, or a massive product catalog, look at Shopify (for ecommerce) or WordPress (for content-heavy sites). If you want a more freeform design editor at a similar price, see our Wix review.

Squarespace vs Wix

This is the most common comparison, and it genuinely depends on priorities. Squarespace wins on design consistency and polish — every template looks intentional. Wix wins on raw design freedom — you can place elements exactly where you want without grid constraints, and Wix has more third-party apps. For most small businesses that don't have a dedicated designer, Squarespace's guardrails actually produce better results. For businesses that want to push layout boundaries, Wix is the better playground. Read our full Wix review for the full breakdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Squarespace have a free plan?

No. Squarespace offers a 14-day free trial (no credit card required), but all paid plans start at around $16/month billed annually. If a free plan is a hard requirement, Wix's free tier or Google Sites may be worth exploring, though both have significant limitations compared to a paid Squarespace plan.

Is Squarespace good for SEO?

Yes, for most small businesses. It auto-generates sitemaps, handles clean URLs, lets you set meta titles and descriptions, includes SSL, and produces fast-loading pages. It won't replace a dedicated SEO platform like Semrush for keyword research and auditing, but it handles on-page fundamentals without additional plugins.

Can I sell products on Squarespace?

Yes. All plans include basic ecommerce. The Business plan charges a 2% transaction fee; Commerce plans eliminate this fee entirely and add advanced merchandising, subscriptions, and more. For stores with hundreds of SKUs or heavy shipping needs, Shopify's ecommerce features are more mature.

Does Squarespace include hosting?

Yes. Every Squarespace plan includes managed hosting, a free SSL certificate, and a free custom domain for the first year. You don't pay a separate hosting bill — it's all bundled into your plan price, which simplifies budgeting considerably.

How is Squarespace's customer support?

Squarespace offers 24/7 live chat support and email support, plus a detailed Help Center and community forums. Phone support is not available. In practice, live chat response times are fast, and the Help Center covers most common issues thoroughly.

Final Verdict

Squarespace earns a 4.6/5 in our 2026 review. It's the right choice when design quality and simplicity matter most — and for photographers, creatives, restaurants, and small service businesses, that's most of what matters. The pricing is fair for what you get (hosting, SSL, SEO, and analytics in one bill), the 14-day free trial removes the risk of trying it, and the AI tools are now genuinely useful for getting started faster.

If you need maximum design freedom or a massive app ecosystem, look at Wix or WordPress instead. But if you want a site that looks professional without needing to hire a designer, Squarespace remains one of the easiest bets in 2026.
