What Is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is OpenAI's consumer and business AI assistant, built on the GPT family of large language models. Launched in November 2022, it became the fastest consumer product to reach 100 million users in history — and by 2026, it remains the most widely recognised AI tool in the world, with over 200 million weekly active users according to OpenAI's published figures.

The product has evolved substantially from its original chat-only format. ChatGPT in 2026 is a multimodal platform: it handles text, images (generation and analysis), voice conversation, file uploads, code execution, and web browsing in a single interface. The underlying GPT-4o model powers most interactions, with access to the o1 and o3 reasoning models available on paid tiers for tasks that benefit from extended, step-by-step thinking — mathematical proofs, complex code reviews, nuanced argument construction.

What's most impressive about ChatGPT is not any single feature but the density of capability packed into one product. The average business user doesn't need a separate image tool, a separate coding assistant, a separate voice interface, and a separate research helper — ChatGPT does all of them competently enough that many users never need to leave the platform.

Key Features

GPT-4o reasoning and writing

GPT-4o is OpenAI's flagship model as of 2026 — fast, multimodal, and capable of handling complex instructions across text, images, and audio in a single conversation turn. According to OpenAI's documentation, GPT-4o is trained on a mix of text and vision data, making it meaningfully more capable at interpreting charts, screenshots, and documents than earlier GPT-4 variants. For everyday writing, summarisation, and analysis tasks, GPT-4o is the reason free-tier users get genuine value without paying.

DALL·E 3 image generation

ChatGPT integrates DALL·E 3 directly, allowing users to generate images from natural language descriptions without switching tools. The feature matters most for marketers, designers, and content creators who want to visualise concepts quickly — whether that's a blog post header, a product mockup concept, or social media graphics. The feature we think matters most here is the ability to iterate images through conversation: describe what to change, and the model adjusts, eliminating the prompt-engineering loop required by standalone image tools.

Custom GPTs

Custom GPTs let users and businesses build task-specific AI assistants that remember instructions, tools, and personas across sessions — without any coding required. OpenAI's GPT Store lists thousands of community-built GPTs covering everything from legal document summarisers to cooking assistants. For businesses, Custom GPTs mean you can pre-load a company's tone of voice, FAQ database, or product catalogue into an AI assistant your team uses consistently. This is the feature most underused by casual users and most valuable to teams.

Voice mode

ChatGPT's Advanced Voice Mode offers real-time spoken conversation with low latency, natural interruption handling, and emotional inflection in responses. According to OpenAI, it processes audio end-to-end rather than converting to text first, which is why it responds to tone and emotion more naturally than text-to-speech alternatives. It's available on iOS and Android, and it's genuinely well-suited to language learning, hands-free drafting, and interview preparation.

Web search and real-time data

ChatGPT Plus and above include integrated web search, letting the model pull current information — news, prices, documentation — rather than relying solely on its training data cutoff. For business users asking about current events, competitor pricing, or recent platform changes, this feature closes a significant gap. The area where ChatGPT still falls short relative to Perplexity is source transparency: ChatGPT's search cites sources, but Perplexity's interface puts source attribution more front-and-centre by design.

File upload and data analysis

Users can upload PDFs, spreadsheets, code files, and images for ChatGPT to read, summarise, and analyse. The built-in code interpreter runs Python in a sandboxed environment, enabling data cleaning, chart generation, and statistical analysis without leaving the chat. For analysts and operations professionals who regularly work with CSV exports and PDF reports, this feature significantly shortens turnaround time on routine analysis tasks.

Canvas document editing

Canvas is OpenAI's collaborative document interface within ChatGPT: a side-by-side editor where the model can write, edit, and refine text or code alongside the user. According to OpenAI's help documentation, Canvas supports targeted edits (highlight a section and ask for a specific change), length adjustment, reading level changes, and adding polish — making it a functional light alternative to Jasper or Notion AI for teams that want document editing without a separate tool subscription.

Who Should Use ChatGPT?

  • Content creators and marketers who need a single tool for writing drafts, generating images, and creating social content — ChatGPT handles all three without switching apps.
  • Developers and technical teams that want AI coding assistance, code review, and debugging baked into their workflow. According to GitHub's 2025 developer survey, ChatGPT was the most-used AI tool for coding outside of dedicated IDE integrations.
  • Small business operators who want a versatile AI assistant for emails, proposals, data analysis, and customer communication — without the complexity or price of purpose-built tools for each task.
  • Educators and students using it for research summaries, essay outlines, problem-solving walkthroughs, and language learning via voice mode.

If your primary use case is deeply technical document processing across 100,000-word files, Claude's context window holds a real advantage. If you need every answer sourced and cited, Perplexity is a better daily driver. ChatGPT's strength is breadth and versatility — the AI for everyone who isn't sure exactly what they need yet.

  • Claude (Anthropic) — better if you value measured, nuanced long-form prose and need to work with very large documents. Claude's 200K token context window handles entire books or research papers. See our AI Platforms comparison →

  • Perplexity — better for research tasks where you need every claim sourced and cited by default. Perplexity builds source attribution into its interface rather than making users hunt for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ChatGPT free to use?

Yes. ChatGPT's free tier gives access to GPT-4o with some usage limits — enough for most casual and moderate business use. Paid plans (Go at $8/month, Plus at $20/month) unlock higher message limits, priority access, and additional features like advanced image generation and voice mode.

What is the difference between ChatGPT Free and Plus?

The free tier runs on GPT-4o with rate limits applied during high demand. ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) removes most rate limits, adds extended thinking (o1 and o3 models), higher file upload limits, deeper image generation capabilities, and priority access. For occasional use, free is sufficient; for daily professional use, Plus or Team is worth the cost.

ChatGPT vs Claude — which is better?

It depends on the task. ChatGPT has the edge on breadth — image generation, voice mode, Custom GPTs, and the largest third-party plugin ecosystem. Claude tends to produce more measured, natural-sounding prose for long-form writing, and its 200K context window handles very large documents better. Most professionals use both: ChatGPT for versatile day-to-day tasks, Claude for nuanced writing and long documents.

Is ChatGPT safe for business use?

OpenAI's Team plan turns off training on your data by default. The Enterprise plan adds SSO, data encryption, and a dedicated data processing agreement. For businesses handling sensitive data, Team or Enterprise is the appropriate tier — not the free or Plus plan, which may use conversations for model improvement unless you opt out in settings.

Final Verdict

ChatGPT earns its rating of 4.8/5 because it does something almost no other tool manages: it is genuinely useful at pop:10 scale — meaning the average person picks it up and gets real value from day one, while professional users unlock depth that keeps them on it for years. The free tier is the most capable free AI product available in 2026. The paid plans are priced fairly for the features they add. And the breadth of what GPT-4o handles — text, images, voice, code, data — makes it the default starting point for anyone building an AI-augmented workflow.

The rating isn't 5/5 because context limits still lag Claude for very large document work, and source transparency lags Perplexity for research-heavy use cases. But as a general-purpose AI assistant for individuals and small teams, nothing matches ChatGPT's combination of accessibility, power, and ecosystem depth.
