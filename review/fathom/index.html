What Is Fathom?

Fathom is an AI-powered meeting recorder and notetaker that works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. It joins your calls automatically, records the audio and video, transcribes the conversation in real time, and generates a structured AI summary with key points, decisions, and action items once the meeting ends.

The core pitch is simple: attend a meeting fully focused on the conversation rather than splitting attention between listening and typing. Fathom handles the documentation layer. Post-meeting, you receive a shareable summary link that teammates, clients, or managers can read in minutes rather than re-watching a full recording. The free plan makes this workflow available to anyone without a budget commitment, which explains its strong word-of-mouth growth since launch.

Key Features

Automatic meeting recording and transcription

Fathom joins meetings as a bot participant, records audio and video, and produces a full transcript. According to Fathom's documentation, transcription is available in 25 languages on all plans. Reviewers on Capterra and GetApp consistently rate transcription accuracy as high for standard English-language calls. Accuracy is lower in noisy environments, with heavy accents, or when speakers overlap — standard limitations for all AI transcription tools.

AI-generated summaries and action items

After each meeting, Fathom generates a structured summary covering the main topics discussed, decisions made, and next steps. The free plan includes basic General and Enhanced summary templates. Advanced summary templates — which provide deeper categorization, more structured action item extraction, and customizable formats — are available on the free tier for up to five calls per month. After that limit, the more basic General template applies. Paid plans remove this cap.

Ask Fathom (AI Q&A)

Paid plans add an "Ask Fathom" feature that lets you query the transcript of any recorded meeting in natural language. For example: "What did Alex commit to on the Q4 roadmap?" or "What was the final decision on the pricing change?" This is particularly useful for reviewing longer calls or searching across a recording library.

CRM and tool integrations

Fathom integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, and Close CRM on paid plans, automatically logging call notes and action items to contact records. This eliminates the manual copy-paste step that most teams find tedious after sales calls. Free plan users can share meeting summaries manually but lack the auto-sync capability.

Meeting highlights and clipping

During a live call, you can mark moments as highlights — a question worth revisiting, a decision being made, a key commitment. These highlights are bookmarked in the recording and surfaced in the summary, so reviewers can jump straight to the most important moments without scrubbing the full video.

Who Should Use Fathom?

  • Freelancers and consultants who want clean meeting summaries to share with clients without the overhead of note-taking during calls.
  • Salespeople and account managers on paid plans — the CRM auto-sync and unlimited advanced summaries make it a genuine productivity multiplier for teams doing 10+ calls a week.
  • Managers and executives who attend recurring team meetings and want an audit trail of decisions and action items without asking an assistant to take notes.
  • Small teams who want to standardize meeting documentation without paying for a heavyweight enterprise platform.

If your primary need is live transcription during a meeting (not just post-meeting summaries), Otter.ai's real-time caption stream or tl;dv's live highlights may serve better in that specific workflow. Fathom's strength is post-meeting summary quality and its free-plan generosity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fathom really free?

Yes — Fathom offers a genuinely usable free plan that includes unlimited meeting recordings, unlimited storage, and unlimited transcription in 25 languages. The key limitation is advanced AI summaries: these are capped at five calls per month on the free tier. After that, only the General/Enhanced summary template is available. For people who attend a few important meetings weekly, the free tier covers most needs.

Does Fathom work with Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?

Yes — Fathom supports Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. The setup process varies slightly by platform, but all three are covered by the free plan.

How accurate is Fathom's transcription?

Fathom's transcription accuracy is generally rated as high by reviewers on platforms like Capterra and GetApp, particularly for English-language calls in quiet environments. Accuracy drops with heavy accents, low-quality microphones, or multiple overlapping speakers — standard limitations across all AI transcription tools.

Does Fathom integrate with HubSpot?

Yes — HubSpot integration is available on the Business plan (~$34/user/month). It automatically logs meeting summaries and action items to HubSpot contact records after each call. Salesforce and Close CRM are also supported on this tier.
