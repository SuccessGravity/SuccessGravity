What Is Heepsy?

Heepsy is an influencer discovery and analytics platform built for brands and agencies that want to identify authentic creators across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Twitter/X. Founded in 2018, Heepsy focuses on the early stages of the influencer marketing workflow — finding the right creators and vetting their audience quality — rather than trying to be an end-to-end campaign management suite. This narrower scope is a deliberate positioning choice: it allows Heepsy to price accessibly and deliver a cleaner, faster search experience than all-in-one enterprise platforms that bundle in features most growing brands don't immediately need.

The platform indexes millions of creator profiles and enriches them with metrics that matter for brand safety and campaign ROI: engagement rate, audience demographics (age, gender, country), estimated reach, and importantly, an audience authenticity score that estimates what percentage of a creator's followers appear to be real, active accounts versus bots, mass followers, or dormant profiles. For brands that have encountered influencer fraud — paying significant fees for campaigns that reached largely fake audiences — this authenticity layer is a meaningful safeguard during the vetting process.

Heepsy's sweet spot is micro-influencer discovery. Its filters are calibrated to let marketers zero in on creators with follower counts in the 5,000–100,000 range across specific niches and geographic markets — exactly the segment that typically has the highest engagement rates and strongest audience trust, but that requires the most discovery effort because these creators don't command the press coverage of top-tier celebrities.

Key Features

Multi-platform creator search with granular filters

Heepsy's core interface is a search engine for creator profiles. The filter set covers platform (Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Twitter/X), follower count range, engagement rate range, location (country, city), language, content category (fashion, beauty, fitness, food, travel, technology, and more), and contact information availability (useful for confirming you can actually reach a creator before adding them to a list). The combination of engagement rate and follower count filters in tandem is the most commonly used pairing for micro-influencer discovery: searching for creators with 10,000–50,000 followers and an engagement rate above 3% in a specific niche surfaces candidates that smaller brands have the budget to partner with and that have demonstrably engaged audiences.

Results display key metrics at a glance — follower count, engagement rate, authenticity score, estimated price range (a rough indicator rather than an offer), and recent content examples — so you can make a preliminary judgment without clicking through to each individual profile.

Audience authenticity scoring

Heepsy assigns each creator an audience authenticity score — a percentage estimate of the proportion of their follower base that appears genuine. The methodology draws on behavioral signals: follower-to-following ratios, account creation patterns, post engagement relative to audience size, and other signals that tend to differentiate real, engaged audiences from bulk follows. According to Heepsy's documentation, accounts scoring above 70% are generally considered to have predominantly authentic audiences, though no automated tool can provide a perfect read on this.

For brands allocating real budget to influencer campaigns, running a basic authenticity check before negotiating fees is table stakes. Heepsy surfaces this data without requiring a separate fraud-detection subscription, which is a meaningful value add at its price point compared to tools that either lack this feature or bundle it only into higher tiers.

Audience demographics breakdown

Beyond the creator's own follower metrics, Heepsy provides demographic breakdowns of each creator's audience: the geographic distribution of followers by country, gender split, and estimated age range. This matters because follower count and engagement rate alone don't tell you whether a creator's audience actually overlaps with your target customer. A fitness creator with 80,000 followers might sound ideal for a US-based supplement brand — but if 60% of their audience is in Brazil or India, the campaign reach in your core market is considerably narrower than the headline number suggests. Heepsy's audience demographic data helps catch these mismatches before a partnership agreement is signed.

Influencer lists and export

Creators can be saved into named lists within Heepsy for campaign planning, team review, or batch outreach. On paid plans, lists can be exported to CSV for further analysis, integration into a CRM, or handoff to a PR or agency team. The export capability matters practically: many brands that use Heepsy for discovery then manage the actual relationship and communications outside the platform — via email or a separate CRM — so clean data export is more important than it might seem from a feature checklist perspective.

Export limits vary by plan tier; Advanced plans allow higher export volumes, which matters for agencies or brands running large-scale discovery passes across multiple campaigns.

Estimated price range indicator

Heepsy displays an estimated price range for each creator based on their follower count and engagement metrics. This is a rough guide — actual rates depend on the creator's niche, content format (feed post vs. story vs. reel vs. YouTube video), exclusivity requirements, and individual negotiation — but it provides a useful order-of-magnitude sense of whether a creator is in a brand's budget range before investing time in an outreach conversation. A nano-influencer with 8,000 followers and an estimated rate of $50–$150 per post occupies a fundamentally different budget category than a mid-tier creator at $1,500–$5,000 per post, and seeing that at a glance during discovery speeds up the shortlisting process.

Who Should Use Heepsy?

Heepsy suits a specific stage of influencer program maturity:

  • Small businesses and startups running their first influencer campaigns who need a cost-effective way to find and vet creators without committing to an enterprise contract.
  • Mid-market brands with a dedicated marketing manager handling influencer relationships manually — Heepsy handles the discovery and vetting, while relationship management happens via email or a lightweight CRM.
  • Agencies managing multiple smaller clients on tighter budgets, where the Business or Advanced tier's export volume and multi-seat access provide enough scale.
  • Brands focused on micro-influencer programs, where the quality of the audience authenticity filter matters more than the breadth of campaign automation.

Brands that have scaled beyond manual relationship management — running 50+ active creator partnerships simultaneously and needing automated outreach, e-commerce attribution, and performance-based payment tracking — will find Heepsy's feature set too limited and should look at Upfluence, Modash, or similar enterprise platforms.

Alternatives

  • Upfluence — The step up from Heepsy for e-commerce brands that need campaign automation, Live Capture, and Shopify revenue attribution. Significantly higher price (custom enterprise contracts), justified for brands running larger programs.
  • Featuring — Mid-market platform with campaign management features beyond discovery. More automation than Heepsy at a higher but still transparent price point.
  • Modash — A Heepsy competitor at similar price ranges, often cited as having a larger database and stronger audience data depth. Worth comparing side-by-side on a trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Heepsy used for?

Heepsy is an influencer discovery and analysis platform. Marketers use it to search for Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Twitter/X creators by niche, location, engagement rate, and audience demographics. Its audience authenticity scoring helps brands avoid partnerships with creators who have inflated follower counts from bot or purchased accounts.

How much does Heepsy cost?

Third-party pricing sources (as of mid-2026) report starter plans from around $49–$69 per month, with Business and Advanced tiers at $149–$169 and $229–$269 per month respectively. Annual billing typically offers a 15–20% discount. Always verify current rates on heepsy.com before purchasing, as pricing has changed multiple times and varies by source.

Does Heepsy have a free plan?

Yes, Heepsy offers a free account for evaluation. The free plan restricts the number of monthly searches and shows only partial profile data — enough to assess the search interface and data quality before committing to a paid tier.

Is Heepsy good for finding micro-influencers?

Yes — Heepsy is particularly suited for micro-influencer discovery. Its filter combination of follower count range, engagement rate minimum, and content category lets you efficiently surface creators in the 1,000–100,000 follower range with genuinely engaged audiences. The authenticity score is especially useful at this tier, where purchased followers are common.

Final Verdict

Heepsy is a practical, accessible influencer discovery tool that solves the core problem — finding authentic creators in a specific niche at a price point most growing brands can justify. Its audience authenticity scoring and demographic filters are genuinely useful for brands that care about partnership quality over raw reach metrics. The limitations are real — no campaign automation, no e-commerce attribution, smaller database than enterprise alternatives — but for brands in the discovery-and-vet phase of their influencer programs, those features are additions rather than gaps.

If your influencer program is currently a spreadsheet and a handful of partnerships per quarter, Heepsy is a meaningful upgrade. When you grow to 50+ active partnerships and need the full campaign management stack, that's the moment to evaluate Upfluence or a similar enterprise platform.
