What Is Klaviyo?

Klaviyo is an email and SMS marketing platform built from the ground up for e-commerce. Founded in 2012 and headquartered in Boston, it has grown to serve over 130,000 businesses — with Shopify brands constituting a large majority of its user base. Klaviyo processes billions of data events per day from connected storefronts: product views, add-to-carts, purchases, refund requests, repeat purchase windows, and more.

What separates Klaviyo from general-purpose email tools isn't the drag-and-drop editor or the template library — those are table stakes in 2026. It's the data model: every contact profile in Klaviyo carries the full order history and behavioral trail from your connected store, and every flow, segment, and campaign can reference that data directly. A "customers who bought X but not Y in the last 90 days" segment that would require a developer elsewhere is a matter of a few filter conditions in Klaviyo.

Key Features

E-commerce flows and automation

Klaviyo's flow builder covers every standard e-commerce lifecycle: welcome series, abandoned-cart recovery, browse abandonment, post-purchase thank-you and upsell, win-back, VIP, and sunset flows. Pre-built templates exist for all of them. Where things get genuinely powerful is in conditional branching: flows can split based on whether a customer bought a specific product, reached a predicted lifetime value threshold, or opened or clicked previous messages. This level of granularity directly affects revenue per recipient, which is the metric that matters most for stores.

Predictive analytics

Klaviyo's predictive analytics features — Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) forecasting, next-order date prediction, and churn risk scoring — are derived from aggregated order data across the platform's user base. According to Klaviyo's documentation, CLV predictions update as new orders come in and use individual store patterns rather than generic benchmarks. This makes features like "high-value predicted customers" segments genuinely useful for targeting rather than vanity metrics. In our assessment, predictive CLV is one of the strongest justifications for Klaviyo over cheaper alternatives for mid-size stores.

Segmentation

Segmentation is where Klaviyo's e-commerce data model pays off most visibly. Properties like total order value, product categories purchased, average order value, and days since last purchase are all natively available. Combined with email engagement signals (open, click, unsubscribe, complaint history), the resulting segments can be remarkably precise — and precise segments mean better deliverability, higher conversion, and fewer wasted sends.

SMS marketing

Klaviyo's SMS add-on runs from the same contact database as email, so you can build flows that coordinate both channels — a cart-abandonment email at hour 1, an SMS nudge at hour 4. SMS is available in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. Pricing is per-message on top of the email plan, which can add up for high-volume transactional programs but stays competitive for promotional sends.

Integrations

The Shopify integration is the tightest in the email marketing category — real-time event sync, pre-built flows that activate on install, and a checkout extension for SMS opt-ins. WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and Magento integrations are solid but require more setup. Klaviyo also connects to 350+ apps including Recharge (subscriptions), Gorgias (customer support), Yotpo (reviews), and Facebook/Google ads for audience sync.

Who Should Use Klaviyo?

  • Shopify brands of any size — the free plan covers the early days; the paid tiers unlock the full power of behavior-driven flows as the list grows.
  • E-commerce stores with repeat purchase cycles — win-back campaigns, post-purchase upsells, and VIP programs all perform significantly better with Klaviyo's purchase-data segmentation than with generic email tools.
  • Brands that want email and SMS in one place — coordinating channels from a single contact profile reduces complexity and improves consistency.
  • Stores migrating off Mailchimp — Klaviyo's import tooling and pre-built Shopify flows make migration straightforward, and the upgrade in e-commerce capability is typically immediate.

If you run a SaaS, coaching business, content newsletter, or B2B lead-generation funnel, Klaviyo's e-commerce architecture is a poor fit. ActiveCampaign, MailerLite, or Kit will serve those use cases better — and often at lower cost for the same list size.

Top Alternatives

  • ActiveCampaign — better for B2B, service businesses, and anyone who needs a full CRM bundled with email automation.
  • Mailchimp — easier to start with, lower cost at small list sizes, but shallower e-commerce segmentation. A fair starting point before migrating to Klaviyo at scale.
  • Mailmodo — better if AMP-powered interactive emails (forms, surveys, and carts embedded inside the email itself) are your priority.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Klaviyo have a free plan?

Yes. Klaviyo's free plan supports up to 250 contacts and 500 email sends per month, plus 150 free SMS/MMS credits. It includes most core features — flows, segmentation, and basic analytics — making it genuinely useful for early-stage stores testing the platform before committing to a paid tier.

Is Klaviyo worth it for a small Shopify store?

For stores doing $5k+ in monthly revenue where email and SMS automation can meaningfully recover abandoned carts and drive repeat purchases, Klaviyo's paid plans typically pay for themselves within the first month. Under that threshold, the free plan is an excellent starting point — you can build your first abandoned-cart flow and welcome series before paying a cent.

Klaviyo vs Mailchimp — which is better for e-commerce?

Klaviyo is the stronger choice for e-commerce. Its Shopify and WooCommerce integrations sync full order history, enabling flows that fire based on what customers actually bought, browsed, or abandoned. Mailchimp's integrations are shallower. If your store's list is under 500 contacts and you mainly send newsletters, Mailchimp's free plan is simpler; beyond that, Klaviyo's e-commerce-native segmentation delivers meaningfully better results.

Does Klaviyo support SMS marketing?

Yes. Klaviyo's SMS add-on lets you send transactional and promotional texts from the same platform as your email campaigns, with shared contact profiles and unified analytics. SMS is available in the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and several other markets. Pricing is per message sent on top of the email plan.

Final Verdict

Klaviyo earns its rating of 4.6/5. For e-commerce brands — particularly those on Shopify — it's the clearest recommendation in the email marketing category in 2026. The combination of real-time store data, behavior-based flows, predictive analytics, and SMS coordination creates a platform where the tool itself actively contributes to revenue. The honest caveat is price: past 10,000 contacts, Klaviyo's costs escalate faster than most alternatives. For non-e-commerce businesses, the architecture is an expensive over-specification. Choose Klaviyo for the store; choose ActiveCampaign or MailerLite for everything else.
