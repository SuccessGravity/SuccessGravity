What Is Murf AI?

Murf AI is a browser-based AI voice generator and production studio founded in 2020. It converts text into natural-sounding voiceovers using a library of over 120 AI voices across 20+ languages and accents. Unlike basic text-to-speech tools, Murf is designed as a full audio production environment: you can sync voiceovers to video and slide presentations, add background music from a built-in library, adjust pitch and speed, and collaborate with teammates in real time.

The platform is especially popular with e-learning designers, YouTube creators, and marketing teams who produce a steady stream of explainer videos, product demos, or training content and want to eliminate the scheduling and cost overhead of recording human narrators.

Key Features

AI voice library (120+ voices, 20+ languages)

Murf's voice library is one of the broadest in the market. Voices are grouped by tone — Conversational, News, Sports, Promo, and more — and you can preview any voice before generating. Most voices sound natural on first pass, and the platform offers granular controls for emphasis, pauses, and pronunciation corrections via a phonetics panel. Quality is consistently "broadcast-ready" for corporate narration, though some voices have a faint synthetic quality on longer passages.

Studio with slide-deck sync

Murf's standout feature is its ability to import a video or a set of slide images and automatically sync the generated voiceover to each slide's timing. You write the script per slide, generate the audio, and the studio aligns playback so that each clip lands exactly where you need it. This removes hours of manual audio-video alignment from e-learning production workflows.

Background music and sound mixer

A built-in royalty-free music library lets you layer background tracks under your voiceover with independent volume control. The mixer isn't as sophisticated as a full DAW, but it handles the most common use case — a subtle ambient track under a product walkthrough — without any additional software.

Team collaboration

On Business and Enterprise plans, multiple users can share projects, leave comments, and hand off work within a shared workspace. Role-based access controls let managers keep a master voice style guide while letting team members produce content independently.

Voice cloning (Enterprise)

Enterprise plans include custom voice cloning: you provide a short recording, and Murf generates a synthetic version of your voice. This is useful for brands that want content to always sound like the same person — a founder, a spokesperson — regardless of who writes the script.

API access

Murf provides an API for developers who want to embed TTS into their own products or automate narration pipelines, available on higher-tier plans. Rate limits and available voices through the API are documented in the developer portal.
