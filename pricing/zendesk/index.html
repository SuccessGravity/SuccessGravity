Questions about Zendesk pricing

How much does Zendesk cost?

Zendesk's cheapest paid plan is listed at $19/agent/month on zendesk.com, checked on October 3, 2026. Prices can vary by region, billing period and promotions, so confirm on the official pricing page before buying.

Does Zendesk have a free plan?

No permanent free plan was listed when we checked zendesk.com on October 3, 2026. Some tools offer a free trial instead.

Is Zendesk priced per user?

Yes. The listed price is per user (seat) per month, so a team of 5 pays roughly five times the listed price. Use the calculator on this page to estimate your total.
