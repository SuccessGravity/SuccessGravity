Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Success Gravity ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit successgravity.com (the "Site"). We designed this Site to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information We Collect

Information you provide

The Site has no user accounts and no sign-up forms. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us — for example, when you email help@successgravity.com. We use that information solely to respond to you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may automatically log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs. We use this data in aggregate to understand site performance and readership; we do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Browser storage

Some interactive features (such as saved filters and "heart" votes on the tool directory) store preferences in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the Site. These services may set cookies and collect usage data under their own privacy policies. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the official browser add-on.

Affiliate Links and Third-Party Cookies

The Site contains affiliate links (see our Affiliate Disclosure). When you click an affiliate link, the affiliate network or the destination company may set cookies on your device to attribute any purchase you make to this Site. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of those third parties, not by this policy. We do not receive personally identifying information about your purchases — typically only anonymized transaction data needed to calculate commissions.

How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help operate the Site (such as hosting and analytics providers), or if required by law.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain data practices. Because we hold almost no personal data, such requests are usually simple — email us at help@successgravity.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

External Links

The Site links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Review their privacy policies before providing them any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact
