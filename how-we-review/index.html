How We Review

Every score on this site comes from the same rubric, applied the same way — here it is.

Our scoring rubric

Every tool receives a score out of 5, built from six weighted criteria:

CriterionWeightWhat we assess
Core capability25%How well the tool does its primary job, tested against realistic tasks — not demo scenarios.
Ease of use20%Time from signup to first real result; quality of onboarding, templates, and documentation.
Value for money20%Pricing against comparable tools, what the entry tier actually includes, and how costs scale.
Support & reliability15%Support channels and hours, response quality, and the tool's uptime and stability record.
Integrations10%Native integrations, API quality, and how well it fits a typical modern stack.
Trust & transparency10%Clear pricing, fair billing practices, data policies, and the vendor's track record.

Our process

  1. Research & shortlist. We track launches, user communities, and category leaders to decide which tools deserve a full review.
  2. Hands-on evaluation. Wherever a trial or free tier exists, we run the same category-standard tasks through the tool and record where it shines or breaks.
  3. Evidence check. We verify claims against documentation, changelogs, and aggregated user feedback — a vendor's marketing page is a starting point, never a source.
  4. Score & verdict. The rubric produces the score; the verdict adds who the tool is best (and wrong) for.
  5. Re-verification. Pricing and key facts are re-checked on a rolling basis — each review and category page shows when its data was last verified.

Independence & affiliate links
