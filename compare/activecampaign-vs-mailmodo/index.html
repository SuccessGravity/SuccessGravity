Quick Verdict

ActiveCampaign wins overall. Its automation builder is far deeper, it ships with a real sales CRM, and its 900+ integrations mean it fits almost any stack. Mailmodo wins if interactive AMP emails are your priority — no other tool in this price range makes it as easy to embed forms, surveys, carts, and bookings directly inside the email, so subscribers convert without ever clicking through to a landing page.

Choosing between ActiveCampaign and Mailmodo is really a choice between two philosophies. ActiveCampaign bets that the money is in what happens after the send — behavior-triggered journeys, lead scoring, and CRM follow-up. Mailmodo bets the money is in the email itself, turning the inbox into an interactive app where subscribers can act on the spot. Email Automation

ActiveCampaign's visual automation builder remains the benchmark in its price class. You can branch journeys on link clicks, site visits, purchases, deal-stage changes, engagement scores, and dozens of other conditions, and its library of 900+ prebuilt recipes means you rarely start from a blank canvas. Split testing entire automation paths — not just subject lines — is the kind of depth most competitors simply don't offer.

Mailmodo covers the fundamentals well. Its journey builder handles welcome series, cart recovery, and drip sequences, and it has a genuinely clever trick: responses submitted inside an AMP email (a survey answer, a form field) can trigger the next step of a journey. But once you need multi-condition branching, scoring, or sales handoffs, you hit its ceiling quickly.

Winner: ActiveCampaign. This is its home turf, and it isn't close.

Interactive & AMP Emails

This category is why Mailmodo exists. Built on Google's AMP for Email framework, it lets you drop interactive widgets — forms, surveys, quizzes, calendars, product carousels, even checkout-style carts — straight into the message. Subscribers book a demo, answer an NPS survey, or complete a purchase without leaving the inbox, and Mailmodo's own case studies routinely show conversion lifts of 2–3x versus click-through emails. It also auto-generates HTML fallbacks for inboxes that don't support AMP, so nothing breaks for Apple Mail users.

ActiveCampaign has no AMP support at all. Its emails are polished, and conditional content blocks add some dynamism, but interaction still means a click out to a landing page.

Winner: Mailmodo, clearly. If in-email conversions are the goal, there's no contest.

CRM & Sales Features

ActiveCampaign includes a genuine sales CRM on Plus plans and above: pipelines, deal tracking, task automation, lead scoring, and win-probability insights. Marketing behavior flows directly into sales context — a lead who binge-reads your pricing page can be scored up and routed to a rep automatically. For small B2B teams, this often replaces a standalone CRM entirely.

Mailmodo has no CRM. It manages contacts, segments, and suppression lists competently, but deal pipelines and sales workflows have to live in another tool (it integrates with HubSpot and others for exactly this reason).

Winner: ActiveCampaign. One platform for marketing and sales versus email-only.

Ease of Use

Mailmodo is the friendlier tool day to day. Its no-code template builder ships with hundreds of prebuilt AMP and HTML templates, the interface stays out of your way, and — remarkably — you can build a fully interactive AMP email without writing a line of code, something that normally requires a developer. Most teams send their first campaign within an hour.

ActiveCampaign is approachable for simple campaigns, but its depth comes with density: lists versus tags, automation goals, scoring rules, and CRM settings take real time to internalize. Budget a few days with the tutorials before it clicks.

Winner: Mailmodo, slightly. Less power to learn means less friction — just know you're trading depth for it.

Deliverability

Both platforms perform well here. ActiveCampaign consistently ranks near the top of independent inbox-placement tests, and Mailmodo's managed sending infrastructure, warmup guidance, and verification tooling produce comparably strong results for typical senders. Neither will save a bad list, but neither will sabotage a good one.

One Mailmodo-specific caveat: to send AMP emails to Gmail users, Google requires sender approval — you register your sending domain and pass a review before AMP content renders. Mailmodo walks you through the process and provides fallbacks in the meantime, but it's an extra hoop ActiveCampaign users never encounter.

Winner: Tie. Both are strong; just plan for Google's AMP approval step if you go with Mailmodo.

Pricing & Value

ActiveCampaign's Starter plan opens at roughly $15/mo for about 1,000 contacts, making it the cheaper entry point — but pricing climbs with both plan tier and list size, and the CRM requires Plus (~$49/mo). Mailmodo starts higher at around $39/mo, but its tiers are simpler and are metered primarily on email credits and contacts, which can work out cheaper for teams that send moderately to mid-sized lists. It also offers a longer trial: 21 days versus ActiveCampaign's 14.

Winner: It depends on your list. Small lists and automation-first teams get more per dollar from ActiveCampaign's entry tier; senders who mainly want interactive campaigns on a predictable bill may find Mailmodo's simpler tiers easier to live with.

Integrations

ActiveCampaign connects to 900+ apps — Shopify, WooCommerce, Stripe, Salesforce, Calendly, every major form and webinar tool, plus Zapier and an open API. Whatever your stack, it almost certainly plugs in natively.

Mailmodo's directory covers the essentials — HubSpot, Shopify, Zapier, webhooks, and a solid API — but sits at roughly 50+ native integrations. Zapier fills many gaps, though that's an extra subscription and an extra point of failure.

Winner: ActiveCampaign. An order-of-magnitude advantage in native connections.

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose ActiveCampaign if…

  • You want deep, behavior-driven automation journeys
  • You need a built-in CRM and lead scoring for sales follow-up
  • Your stack depends on lots of native integrations
  • You're starting with a small list and a ~$15/mo budget
  • You want a platform you won't outgrow in a year

Choose Mailmodo if…

  • Interactive AMP emails and in-email conversions are the priority
  • You run surveys, bookings, or feedback loops by email
  • You want the easiest possible no-code email builder
  • You already have a CRM and just need a sending platform
  • You prefer simpler pricing tiers and a longer 21-day trial

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use ActiveCampaign and Mailmodo together?

Yes, and some teams do exactly that: ActiveCampaign as the system of record for automation and CRM, with Mailmodo handling interactive AMP campaigns like surveys and booking emails. Contacts can be synced via Zapier or webhooks, though maintaining two sending tools adds cost and complexity most small teams won't want.

Do AMP emails work in every inbox?

No. AMP rendering is supported by Gmail and Yahoo Mail (and requires Google sender approval for Gmail), while Apple Mail and Outlook show a fallback. Mailmodo automatically generates an HTML fallback version of every campaign, so non-AMP recipients still get a normal, clickable email.

Does either platform have a free plan?

No — both are trial-only. ActiveCampaign offers a 14-day free trial and Mailmodo offers 21 days, neither requiring a credit card. If a permanently free tier is essential, you'd need to look at tools like Mailchimp or Brevo instead.

Which is better for ecommerce stores?

ActiveCampaign for most stores, thanks to native Shopify and WooCommerce integrations, abandoned-cart automations, and purchase-based segmentation. Mailmodo is worth a look if in-email product carousels and cart widgets fit your strategy — but you'll likely pair it with other tools for lifecycle automation.

Final Verdict

ActiveCampaign (4.6/5) takes the overall win on the strength of its automation depth, built-in CRM, and integration ecosystem — it's simply the more complete growth platform, and our full ActiveCampaign review explains why it remains our top automation pick for 2026. Mailmodo (4.4/5) is the specialist's choice: if converting subscribers inside the inbox is your core play, its AMP toolkit is the best we've tested, and our Mailmodo review covers it in full. Both offer free trials with no credit card required, so the smartest move is to run your next real campaign through the one that matches your priority above.
