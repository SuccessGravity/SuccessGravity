☁️ Best Cloud & Infrastructure Platforms in 2026

AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Cloudflare, Vercel, and DigitalOcean compared — where to run your apps, sites, and data in 2026.

Last updated: September 2026 · 6 tools compared · Independently scored

Cloud platforms are where modern businesses run everything: websites, apps, databases, and AI workloads. The big three (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure) offer hundreds of services at global scale, while developer-focused platforms (Vercel, DigitalOcean) trade breadth for simplicity, and Cloudflare makes any site faster and safer from the edge.

Startups and small teams usually move fastest on the simpler platforms — predictable pricing and less to configure. Choose a big-three provider when you need specific managed services, enterprise compliance, or credits from their startup programs. And almost every site benefits from Cloudflare's free tier in front of it, regardless of where it's hosted.

What to look for

  • Pricing predictability — the #1 cloud complaint
  • Managed services you need (databases, AI, queues)
  • Free tiers and startup credit programs
  • Egress fees and lock-in when you leave

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best cloud tool in 2026?

Based on our scoring, the top-rated picks are Cloudflare, AWS, and Vercel. Cloudflare leads at 4.7/5 — make any website faster and safer with a global CDN, free SSL, DDoS protection, and an edge developer platform. The right choice still depends on your budget and team size, which is why we compare them side by side above.

How much do cloud tools cost?

Among the tools we track in this category, entry pricing ranges from about $4/month to $20/month. Most vendors tier pricing by usage or seats, so real cost depends on your scale — check each tool's pricing page for current rates (last checked September 2026).

Which cloud is best for a small business or startup?

For a website or SaaS MVP, start simple: Vercel or DigitalOcean for hosting with Cloudflare in front is cheap and hard to outgrow quickly. Pick AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure when you need their specific managed services or enterprise compliance — and apply to their startup credit programs before you pay retail.
