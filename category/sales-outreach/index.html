🎯 Sales Prospecting & Outreach Tools

Lead databases, cold email and sales engagement tools — entry prices checked on each vendor's own pricing page.

Last updated: October 2026 · 13 tools · Prices checked at the source

Prospecting tools do two jobs: finding the right people to contact (lead databases and email finders) and reaching them at a sensible pace (cold email and sales engagement). Many small teams only need one of the two, so it pays to be clear about which problem you're solving before you subscribe.

Pricing in this category usually scales with credits (contacts you can look up), mailboxes, or seats. A low entry price can rise quickly once you add more senders or need more lookups each month, so compare the plan limits, not just the headline number.

What to look for

  • Data source and accuracy: where contact data comes from and how bounces are handled
  • Credit or send limits on the plan you can actually afford
  • Deliverability help: warm-up, sending limits, and domain setup guidance
  • CRM sync, so replies and meetings land in your pipeline
