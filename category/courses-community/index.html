🎓 Courses & Communities Tools

Platforms for selling courses, running paid communities and memberships — entry prices and free plans compared.

Last updated: October 2026 · 6 tools · Prices checked at the source

Course and community platforms let creators and experts sell what they know: recorded courses, live cohorts, paid memberships, or a community space. Some focus on courses, some on community, and several now do both.

Watch for transaction fees on lower plans: a platform with a cheap monthly price but a percentage cut of sales can cost more than a pricier plan once you're selling regularly.

What to look for

  • Transaction fees on each plan, not just the monthly price
  • Course, community, or both
  • Checkout, payment plans, and coupons built in
  • Whether members need a separate app
