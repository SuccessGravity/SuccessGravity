💰 Best Business Loans & Funding in 2026

Compare small-business loan marketplaces and funding platforms in 2026 — loan types, speed, and what to check before applying.

Last updated: September 2026 · 5 tools compared · Independently scored

Online lending marketplaces match small businesses with term loans, lines of credit, SBA loans, and revenue-based funding — often with decisions in days instead of the weeks banks take. Marketplaces like LendingTree and BestLoanUSA let you compare multiple offers from one application.

Before applying, know your credit profile, time in business, and monthly revenue — these three factors determine which products you'll qualify for. Compare APR (not just the payment), origination fees, and prepayment terms. A marketplace soft-pull comparison protects your credit score while you shop.

What to look for

  • Soft credit pull for initial offers
  • Full APR disclosure, not just payment amounts
  • Funding speed vs. cost trade-off
  • Prepayment penalties and origination fees

What credit score do I need for a small business loan?

Requirements vary by product: SBA and bank term loans typically want 680+, online term loans often approve 600+, and revenue-based financing may accept lower scores if monthly revenue is strong. Marketplaces are useful precisely because they match your profile to lenders likely to approve it. This is general information, not financial advice — review terms carefully before signing.
