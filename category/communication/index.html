📞 Phone, Video & Team Chat Tools

Business phone numbers, video meetings, team chat and meeting assistants — with entry prices from each vendor.

Last updated: October 2026 · 14 tools · Prices checked at the source

Communication tools are often the biggest per-seat line in a small business's software bill, because everyone on the team needs them. Business phone systems give you a shared number, call routing and texting; video and chat tools keep the team connected; meeting assistants record and summarize calls.

Before adding a new tool, check what you already pay for: office suites such as Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 include video meetings and chat, and that can make a separate subscription unnecessary.

What to look for

  • Per-seat price at your team size, not the single-user price
  • Phone: local and toll-free numbers, call routing, and texting rules in your country
  • Meetings: participant limits, time limits on free plans, and recording
  • Overlap with what your office suite already includes
