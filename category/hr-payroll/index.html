🧑‍💼 HR, Payroll & Time Tracking Tools

Payroll, HR, global hiring and time tracking tools for small teams — entry prices from each vendor's own site.

Last updated: October 2026 · 59 tools · Prices checked at the source

Payroll and HR software usually charges a base fee plus a per-person fee, so the real cost depends on headcount. Time tracking tools are cheaper and often per-seat, and they're commonly used by agencies and freelancers to bill clients accurately.

If you hire contractors or employees abroad, employer-of-record and contractor platforms handle contracts and payments across countries, at a higher per-person price than domestic payroll.

What to look for

  • Total monthly cost at your headcount (base fee + per person)
  • Automatic payroll tax filing for your state or country
  • Contractor vs employee support
  • Integrations with your accounting software
