🏛️ Best Free Government & Business Resources in 2026

SBA.gov, SCORE, Grants.gov, USPTO and more — the official free resources every US small business should know about.

Last updated: September 2026 · 6 tools compared · Independently scored

Some of the most valuable business tools aren't products at all — they're official government and non-profit resources that most owners never discover. SBA.gov backs loans and publishes plain-English startup guides, SCORE pairs you with an experienced volunteer mentor for free, Grants.gov lists every federal grant, and USPTO is where you protect the name you're building.

These sites charge nothing and sell nothing, which is exactly why nobody advertises them to you. Bookmark the ones that match your stage: starting up (SBA, SCORE), funding (Grants.gov, SBA loan programs), protecting your work (USPTO), and taxes (IRS Small Business). One afternoon exploring them can save real money on services you'd otherwise pay for.

What to look for

  • Official .gov or established non-profit source
  • Programs that match your business stage
  • Free mentoring and counseling offers
  • Local chapters or district offices near you

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best gov & free resources tool in 2026?

Based on our scoring, the top-rated picks are SBA.gov, SCORE, and IRS Small Business. SBA.gov leads at 4.7/5. The right choice still depends on your budget and team size, which is why we compare them side by side above.

Are these government resources really free to use?

Yes — everything here is taxpayer-funded or non-profit: guides, mentoring, grant listings, and tax resources cost nothing. Watch out for third-party sites that charge for 'help' filing things you can file yourself for free on the official site; always start at the .gov domain.
