💚 Free Tools Every Business Owner
Should Bookmark

30+ genuinely free tools to measure, market, and run your business — no expiring trials, no catch. Curated and kept current.

Last updated: September 2026 · Press Ctrl/Cmd + D to bookmark this page

Most "free tools" lists are padded with 14-day trials. This one isn't. Everything below is either free forever or has a free tier a real business can actually live on — and the first section alone (analytics, Search Console, Clarity) is the setup we'd install on any new business site before spending a dollar on marketing.

📊 Measure What's Happening

Install these first — you can't improve what you can't see. All six are free forever.

Google Analytics 4 💚 100% Free

Who visits your site, where they came from, and what they did. The measurement backbone of every online business.

Pro tip: Set up "key events" for your contact form and purchases in week one — traffic alone tells you nothing.

Open →

Google Search Console 💚 100% Free

Your Google rankings, the exact queries people typed, and indexing alerts — data straight from Google.

Pro tip: Check the Queries report monthly for keywords ranking #8–#20, then improve those pages: fastest SEO wins available.

Open →

Bing Webmaster Tools 💚 100% Free

The same idea for Bing — which also feeds ChatGPT and Copilot search results. Imports your GSC setup in one click.

Open →

Microsoft Clarity 💚 100% Free

Watch real recordings of visitors using your site, plus heatmaps and rage-click detection. Unlimited and free.

Pro tip: Watch 10 session recordings of your checkout or contact page — you will find at least one thing to fix.

Open →

Google Tag Manager 💚 100% Free

Add analytics and ad pixels without editing code — marketers manage tags, developers stay out of it.

Open →

Looker Studio 💚 100% Free

Turn GA4 + Search Console + Ads data into one shareable live dashboard. Google's free BI tool.

Ahrefs Webmaster Tools 💚 100% Free

Ahrefs' backlink data and site audit, free for sites you own — the closest thing to a free Ahrefs subscription.
