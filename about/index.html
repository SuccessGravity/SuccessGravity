Why I Built Success Gravity

Hi — I'm David, a marketer and business owner. Success Gravity started with a habit I couldn't shake: for years, friends and fellow business owners kept asking me the same questions, and I kept giving the same answers. "Install this. Try that site. Yes, that subscription is actually worth it." Most people around me didn't even know these tools existed — tools I used every day to run marketing campaigns and grow real businesses.

At some point the answer became obvious: stop repeating myself and put it all in one place. Not just a list of names, but the comparison I'd give a friend — what each tool actually does, what it really costs, where it falls short, and which one I'd pick in their situation. That's Success Gravity: the recommendation engine I wish I could have handed to every person who ever asked me "so what should I use?"

Yes, some links here earn us a commission — that's disclosed everywhere it applies, and it keeps the site free. But the site exists because the advice came first. The tools I recommend most enthusiastically include plenty that pay us nothing at all — including the completely free stack I tell every new business owner to set up before spending a dollar.

How We Review

Our reviews follow a consistent framework so tools can be compared fairly:

  • Core job: What is this tool actually best at — and does it deliver?
  • Real pricing: What it costs at realistic usage levels, including the gotchas (per-seat fees, contact-based tiers, add-ons).
  • Ease of use: How long it takes to get from signup to real results.
  • Honest cons: Every tool has trade-offs. We say what they are, even for tools we recommend.
  • Who it's for: A tool that's perfect for an agency may be overkill for a solo creator. We always say who should buy — and who shouldn't.
