How we check prices

We read each vendor’s own pricing page, help center or announcement — never a third-party summary — and link it next to every entry. If a page can’t be read, the tool stays “not yet verified” rather than guessed.

Announced means the vendor published a change on that date. Listing corrected means our listed price was out of date and we fixed it on that date; the vendor may have changed it earlier.
