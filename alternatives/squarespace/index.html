Best Squarespace Alternatives in 2026 (Compared)

Squarespace is beautiful — but its Personal plan starts at ~$16/month, there's no free plan, and the template lock-in means you can't switch designs after launch. These six alternatives deliver polished websites (and in most cases, more flexibility) for less.

Why Look for a Squarespace Alternative?

Squarespace earned its reputation by making beautiful websites accessible to non-designers. Its award-winning templates, cohesive design system, and all-in-one billing (hosting, SSL, CDN, and templates in one subscription) have made it a go-to for photographers, creative professionals, and design-conscious small businesses since the early days of website builders. The platform's visual quality is genuine — its Personal plan (~$16/mo, billed annually) gives you a genuinely polished site out of the box, with no technical setup required.

The reasons people look elsewhere tend to cluster around three things. First, pricing: Squarespace has no free plan, and its Business plan (~$23/mo) is required before you can run third-party payment processors or add advanced blocks. Second, flexibility: unlike Wix's freeform drag-and-drop editor, Squarespace uses a section-and-block structure where elements snap to predefined positions — it's elegant but constrained. Third, template commitment: once you publish on a Squarespace template, you cannot switch to a different one without rebuilding your content. The alternatives below each address at least one of these pain points, and several address all three.

1. Wix — Best Overall Squarespace Alternative for Design Flexibility

★★★★★ 4.4/5 · From ~$17/mo (Free plan available)

Wix is the most direct Squarespace alternative for the widest range of users. Where Squarespace holds you to a section-and-block layout, Wix's editor is a genuine free-form canvas: drag any element anywhere on the page, with pixel-level control over placement and spacing. Its template library of 900+ designs covers every category Squarespace serves — portfolios, service businesses, restaurants, online stores, blogs, events — and in most cases offers more variety within each category. Wix's free plan lets you build and publish a site with Wix branding before spending a cent, and its paid plans start at roughly $17/month for a custom domain and no ads. For photographers and creative professionals who've found Squarespace's block system too rigid, Wix's editor gives back the control Squarespace removes. One important Wix caveat: unlike Squarespace, you cannot switch templates after the site is published, so choose your starting template thoughtfully.

2. WordPress.com — Best for Bloggers and Content-Heavy Sites

★★★★★ 4.3/5 · Free / From ~$9/mo

WordPress.com is Squarespace's most logical content-first alternative. Built on the same open-source CMS that powers roughly 43% of the web, WordPress.com combines the ease of a hosted platform with the content management depth of the world's most used blogging engine. Its Starter plan (~$9/mo, annual) includes a custom domain, no ads, 6GB storage, and access to dozens of block-based themes through the Gutenberg editor. For businesses where writing, publishing, and content SEO are central — consultants, writers, media sites, local businesses that blog regularly — WordPress.com's editorial tools are deeper than Squarespace's. The Block editor gives good page design flexibility, and the platform's plugin ecosystem (available on Business and higher plans, ~$25/mo) is unmatched for adding custom functionality. The gap versus Squarespace: WordPress.com's designs require more manual configuration to reach Squarespace's visual polish level, and ecommerce is less integrated at the lower plans.

3. Webflow — Best for Designers Who Want Code-Level Control Without Code

★★★★★ 4.6/5 · Free plan / From ~$14/mo

Webflow occupies a unique position: it gives designers control over layout, animations, interactions, and CMS structure that approaches what a custom-coded site provides — without writing HTML or CSS directly. Its visual editor generates clean, semantic code under the hood, which benefits both performance and SEO. For agencies, freelance designers, SaaS marketing sites, and businesses with sophisticated design requirements, Webflow creates results that simply aren't achievable in Squarespace's constrained block structure. Its CMS is particularly powerful: you can define custom content types (staff, case studies, testimonials, products) and build reusable layouts that pull from them automatically — a workflow Squarespace's CMS doesn't match. The trade-off is a steeper learning curve than Squarespace or Wix. Webflow rewards the investment, but expect to spend real time learning its mental model. Its free plan lets you build on a Webflow.io subdomain; paid plans start at around $14/mo for a custom domain with a simple website, and the Site plans scale from there based on page count and CMS items.

4. Hostinger — Best Budget-Friendly Squarespace Alternative with AI Features

★★★★★ 4.4/5 · From ~$2.49/mo

Hostinger is the most affordable option on this list by a significant margin — its website builder plans start at roughly $2.49/month (promotional rate; renews higher, verify on Hostinger's site) and include an AI website generator that builds a complete site from a brief description in seconds, an AI logo maker, 150+ templates, and built-in SEO tools. For individuals and small businesses where budget is the primary constraint — anyone for whom Squarespace's ~$16/month minimum feels steep — Hostinger's combination of AI-assisted setup and low entry price is hard to match. The design output isn't quite at Squarespace's level of visual polish, and the editor has less flexibility than Wix's freeform canvas, but for a business card website, local services site, or personal portfolio at minimal cost, Hostinger delivers real value. Its web hosting plans (separate from the website builder) are among the best-value shared hosting options available, which also makes it a natural home if you eventually want to move to self-hosted WordPress.

5. Shopify — Best Squarespace Alternative if Ecommerce is Your Priority

★★★★★ 4.8/5 · From ~$29/mo

If the reason you're leaving Squarespace is that your online store is growing beyond what its Commerce plans handle well, Shopify is the logical destination. Squarespace Commerce (from ~$28/mo) is capable for small catalogs and simple selling, but Shopify's ecommerce infrastructure at $29/month already exceeds what Squarespace offers at any price: 8,000+ apps, the highest-converting checkout in the industry (Shop Pay accelerated checkout), multi-location inventory, real-time carrier shipping rates, B2B features, and the ability to sell across 100+ countries. Where Shopify trails Squarespace is in pure design aesthetics — Squarespace's templates are more visually polished out of the box, and its editorial layout tools are easier for non-ecommerce content. If you have a growing product catalog, outgrowing Squarespace Commerce is the trigger to move. If the website experience is the core of your brand and you sell a small catalog, Squarespace's Commerce plans are genuinely competitive.

6. GoDaddy Website Builder — Fastest Setup for Local and Service Businesses

★★★★★ 4.1/5 · From ~$9.99/mo

GoDaddy's website builder sits in a niche that's rarely discussed: fastest time-to-live for local and service businesses that need a professional presence online without spending hours on design. GoDaddy's AI-assisted setup generates a complete, mobile-optimized website from a business type, name, and location in minutes — including sections for services, booking, reviews, and contact. Its integration with GoDaddy's domain and email hosting means everything — domain registration, website, professional email — can be managed from one account. At roughly $9.99/month, it undercuts Squarespace's Personal plan considerably. The trade-off is design flexibility: GoDaddy's templates are more templated (less customizable) than Squarespace or Wix, and the editor's control over layout details is more limited. For a plumber, landscaper, dental practice, or local retailer that wants a professional website that ranks in Google, books appointments, and doesn't require ongoing management, GoDaddy's simplicity is a feature, not a limitation.

Which Squarespace Alternative Should You Choose?

  • Best overall alternative: Wix — covers all of Squarespace's use cases with more design freedom, a free plan, and a comparable starting price.
  • Best for content and blogging: WordPress.com — deeper publishing tools, SEO control, and the world's largest blogging ecosystem.
  • Best for designers and agencies: Webflow — the only no-code tool that gives you code-level design control with a visual interface.
  • Best budget option: Hostinger — the lowest price on the list, with AI-assisted site generation that gets you online in minutes.
  • Best for growing ecommerce: Shopify — when selling products online is the business, not an add-on.
  • Best for local/service businesses: GoDaddy — fastest setup, lowest management overhead, all-in-one domain and email billing.

A note on staying with Squarespace: if you've already built a polished Squarespace site and you're primarily frustrated with pricing, consider whether Squarespace's annual billing (which reduces costs relative to monthly) addresses the issue before migrating. Website migrations always involve more time than anticipated, and Squarespace's template quality is real value that the total cost calculation should include.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free Squarespace alternative?

Yes. Wix offers a free plan with Wix branding (no custom domain), which is enough to test whether Wix fits your needs before committing to a paid plan from ~$17/mo. WordPress.com has a free plan as well. GoDaddy offers a free trial. Hostinger's plans start at roughly $2.49/mo — the most affordable paid option on this list. If a completely free website builder is essential, Wix's free tier is the most fully-featured option, though the Wix branding makes it unsuitable for professional or business use until you upgrade.

What is the best Squarespace alternative for photographers and creatives?

Wix is the most direct Squarespace alternative for photographers and portfolio sites. Its template library has strong photography-specific designs, and the drag-and-drop editor gives the same visual control as Squarespace with more layout flexibility. Webflow is worth considering if you want finer design control and don't mind a steeper learning curve — its CMS and animation capabilities are unmatched in the no-code category. If you primarily sell prints or digital downloads, Squarespace's Commerce plans are actually competitive with Shopify at that scale.

Can I export my Squarespace site to another platform?

Squarespace allows you to export certain content — blog posts, pages, and gallery images — as XML files that some platforms (WordPress.com, WordPress.org) can import. Product data from Squarespace's Commerce plans can be exported as CSV files. However, your page designs, custom CSS, and URL structure will not transfer automatically — a migration to any new platform involves rebuilding the site's layout. For small sites (under 20 pages), this is a few hours of work. For large sites or complex stores, factor in a full day or more.

Is Squarespace worth it compared to cheaper alternatives?

Squarespace's templates are genuinely among the most polished in the website builder industry, and its all-in-one pricing (hosting, security, CDN, templates) simplifies the billing stack. The question is whether you use the design quality it delivers. For a photography portfolio, boutique hotel site, or high-end service business where visual sophistication is part of the brand, Squarespace's premium feel justifies the premium price. For a standard business website or a growing ecommerce store, Wix (~$17/mo) or Hostinger (~$2.49/mo) deliver most of what Squarespace offers at a lower cost — verify current pricing on each vendor's site before deciding.
