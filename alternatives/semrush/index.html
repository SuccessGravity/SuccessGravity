What Is Semrush — and Why Look for Alternatives?

Semrush is one of the most widely used SEO and digital marketing platforms in the world. Launched in 2008, it has grown into a 55+ tool suite covering keyword research, competitive analysis, backlink auditing, on-page SEO, content marketing, social media tracking, and paid advertising intelligence. For agencies and in-house SEO teams that need a single platform to manage and report on all aspects of search visibility, Semrush is often the default recommendation. The keyword database alone claims over 25 billion keywords across 140 geographic databases, making it among the most comprehensive research tools available. You can read our full Semrush review for a deeper look at what it offers.

Despite its depth, Semrush is not the right fit for everyone. The Pro plan starts at around $120/month (billed monthly), and the Guru plan — which unlocks content marketing tools, historical data, and multi-device tracking — climbs to $230+/month. For freelancers, early-stage startups, or businesses with focused needs like local SEO or pure keyword research, that price tag is hard to justify. I've spoken with dozens of independent consultants who told me they needed maybe 20% of what Semrush offers, and they were paying 100% of the price. Beyond cost, some users find the platform overwhelming: the feature set is genuinely massive, and onboarding new team members takes time. Others simply have a specific workflow need — say, white-label reporting or content optimization scoring — where a specialist tool does the job better.

Why People Switch from Semrush

Based on what I hear from SEO practitioners and what patterns show up in community forums, the main reasons people look for Semrush alternatives fall into three buckets:

  • Price: At $120–$230+/month, Semrush is a significant line item for individuals and small teams. Many alternatives offer 70–80% of the core functionality at a fraction of the cost.
  • Feature overlap and complexity: Semrush bundles many tools into one subscription. If you only use keyword research and rank tracking, you may be paying for social media monitoring, PPC research, and content templates you never open.
  • Specific niche needs: Local SEO professionals often prefer tools with stronger local SERP tracking. Content writers frequently gravitate toward tools like Surfer SEO that center the workflow on content scoring rather than raw keyword data. Agencies with white-label reporting requirements may find SE Ranking a better fit.

1. Ahrefs — Best for Backlink Analysis and Site Audit

Starting at ~$129/mo  |  Free limited tools available

Ahrefs is the closest direct competitor to Semrush in terms of breadth and data quality, and for many SEO professionals it is actually the preferred tool — particularly for link building and backlink research. The Ahrefs link index is frequently cited as one of the most comprehensive available, and the Site Explorer tool gives you deep visibility into any domain's backlink profile, organic keyword rankings, and traffic estimates. When I run backlink audits for clients, Ahrefs is consistently the tool I reach for first.

Key strengths vs Semrush: Ahrefs' backlink data is generally considered more comprehensive than Semrush's for link research. The Site Audit tool is fast and reliable. Content Explorer lets you find high-performing content in any niche by search volume, referring domains, or social shares. The Keywords Explorer has a clean interface with strong SERP analysis overlays. Ahrefs also introduced a free suite of standalone tools (Ahrefs Webmaster Tools) that provides meaningful data for site owners without a paid subscription.

Pricing: Ahrefs Lite starts at ~$129/month, which is slightly above Semrush Pro's starting price, making it more of a peer competitor than a budget alternative. However, the data quality and interface are strong enough that many practitioners pay the premium over Semrush specifically.

Best for: Link builders, SEO agencies, and technical SEO practitioners who prioritize backlink depth and site audit accuracy over the breadth of Semrush's marketing suite features.

2. Moz Pro — Best for Beginners and Local SEO

Starting at ~$99/mo  |  30-day free trial

Moz Pro is one of the oldest names in the SEO tool space, and it has maintained a loyal following by prioritizing usability and approachability over raw feature count. The interface is cleaner and more intuitive than Semrush's, which makes it a natural recommendation for beginners, small business owners, and anyone who finds Semrush's depth overwhelming. The 30-day free trial is the most generous in this category — enough time to run real projects and evaluate the data quality against your actual sites.

Key strengths vs Semrush: Moz invented the Domain Authority (DA) metric, which remains the most widely referenced domain-strength indicator in SEO conversations. The Keyword Explorer's Difficulty score and Priority metric are genuinely useful for planning. Local SEO capabilities are strong: Moz Local (a separate product) handles listing management across major directories, making Moz a cohesive choice for brick-and-mortar businesses. The Link Explorer is solid for basic link analysis, and the on-page optimization recommendations are clearly explained for non-technical users.

Pricing: Moz Pro Standard starts at ~$99/month, which is meaningfully cheaper than Semrush Pro and comes with a 30-day trial. Annual billing brings the cost down further.

Best for: In-house marketers new to SEO, small businesses managing local search presence, and anyone who values clean UX and educational resources over raw data depth.

3. SE Ranking — Best Budget Option with White-Label

Starting at ~$52/mo  |  14-day free trial

SE Ranking has grown significantly in 2025–2026 and is now one of the most compelling full-suite Semrush alternatives for budget-conscious users and agencies. At around $52/month for the Essential plan, you get rank tracking, keyword research, on-page SEO auditing, backlink monitoring, and competitor analysis — core capabilities that would cost more than twice as much on Semrush. In my view, SE Ranking delivers the best price-to-feature ratio among the tools in this comparison.

Key strengths vs Semrush: SE Ranking's white-label reporting is a standout feature for agencies — you can fully brand client reports with your own logo and domain, a capability Semrush only offers on its Agency Growth Kit at significant additional cost. The rank tracker is accurate and supports daily tracking at lower price points. The keyword research module is solid for most use cases. SE Ranking also added a content marketing module and a competitor analysis tool that have improved meaningfully in recent versions.

Pricing: Essential plan ~$52/month; Pro plan ~$95/month with more tracked keywords and projects. Annual billing reduces costs further. White-label reporting is included on higher plans.

Best for: Freelance SEO consultants, small digital agencies who need white-label reporting, and budget-focused teams who want a full-suite tool without Semrush's price tag.

4. Mangools — Best for Keyword Research on a Budget

Starting at ~$29/mo  |  10-day free trial

Mangools is a suite of five focused SEO tools — KWFinder, SERPChecker, SERPWatcher, LinkMiner, and SiteProfiler — packaged together at a price that undercuts Semrush by a wide margin. The suite doesn't try to replicate everything Semrush does; instead, it does keyword research, SERP analysis, and rank tracking very well and keeps the interface refreshingly simple. If your primary need is finding low-competition keywords and tracking how you rank for them, Mangools covers that workflow at roughly a quarter of Semrush's cost.

Key strengths vs Semrush: KWFinder is one of the cleanest keyword research interfaces available — the keyword difficulty score is visual and intuitive, and the SERP analysis for each keyword shows the exact metrics (DA, PA, backlinks, social shares) for every ranking URL, which is immediately actionable. SERPChecker's competitor overlap view is useful for quickly spotting content gaps. The tool is genuinely beginner-friendly without sacrificing data accuracy for core metrics.

Pricing: Mangools Basic starts at ~$29/month (or ~$19.90/month billed annually), making it the most affordable paid option in this comparison. The 10-day free trial doesn't require a credit card.

Best for: Bloggers, content marketers, and solo practitioners who need solid keyword research and rank tracking without the complexity or cost of a full SEO suite.

5. Surfer SEO — Best for Content Optimization and GEO

Starting at ~$89/mo  |  7-day free trial

Surfer SEO takes a fundamentally different approach from the other tools on this list: it is built around content optimization and generative engine optimization (GEO) rather than backlink analysis or rank tracking. Where Semrush gives you a keyword and tells you how competitive it is, Surfer opens the top-ranking content, analyzes it for NLP terms, heading structure, word count, and entity coverage, and then gives your content a real-time score as you write. For content teams and SEO writers, this on-page intelligence is transformative. See our full Surfer SEO review for a detailed breakdown of the platform.

Key strengths vs Semrush: Surfer's Content Editor is the most actionable content optimization tool in the market — writing to a Surfer outline and scoring your draft against the top results is a measurably different workflow from using Semrush's SEO Writing Assistant. The SERP Analyzer provides granular on-page data across the entire first page. The Keyword Research tool clusters related terms automatically, which speeds up content planning. In 2025–2026, Surfer added strong GEO features focused on optimizing for AI Overview appearances and answer engine results, which Semrush has not yet matched.

Pricing: Essential plan ~$89/month; Scale plan ~$129/month with more articles and AI features. The 7-day free trial is credit-card required but fully featured.

Best for: Content strategists, SEO writers, and teams whose primary workflow centers on producing optimized content rather than backlink outreach or technical SEO auditing.

6. Ubersuggest — Best Free/Cheap Option for Basics

Starting at ~$12/mo  |  Free plan available

Ubersuggest, built by Neil Patel, is the most accessible entry point in this comparison. The free plan gives you a meaningful number of daily keyword searches, a site audit, and basic backlink data — enough to get started without any payment. The paid plans are priced aggressively at around $12/month (or a one-time lifetime payment option), making it by far the cheapest paid option here. The data quality doesn't match Ahrefs or Semrush, but for small websites, early-stage SEO campaigns, and users who are just learning the basics, it does the job.

Key strengths vs Semrush: Price is the obvious lead. The interface is clean and beginner-friendly. The AI writing assistant built into paid plans adds utility for content creation. The Chrome extension provides quick on-SERP keyword data without opening a separate dashboard. For a solopreneur or small business owner managing their own SEO, the free plan alone covers most routine keyword research tasks.

Pricing: Free plan with daily limits; Individual plan ~$12/month; Business plan ~$20/month. Lifetime deals are occasionally offered on the Ubersuggest website, which can make it extremely cost-effective for long-term users.

Best for: Bloggers, solopreneurs, and small business owners who want basic keyword data and site insights without a subscription commitment, or as a supplementary tool alongside a primary SEO platform.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free alternative to Semrush?

Ubersuggest offers the most capable free tier of any Semrush alternative — it includes limited keyword lookups, a site audit, and backlink data. Google Search Console is also free and provides first-party ranking data directly from Google, making it a complementary must-have regardless of which paid tool you choose.

Is Ahrefs better than Semrush?

For backlink analysis, Ahrefs is widely considered the best in the market — its link index is frequently cited as the most comprehensive available. Semrush has a larger keyword database and stronger competitive intelligence features. Which is "better" depends on whether your priority is link building (Ahrefs) or keyword research and competitive analysis (Semrush).

What is the cheapest Semrush alternative with good data?

SE Ranking starts at around $52/month and offers full-suite SEO capabilities including keyword research, backlink tracking, rank tracking, and site audit. Mangools is even cheaper at around $29/month and is excellent for keyword research and SERP analysis, though it has a narrower feature set than SE Ranking or Semrush.

Can I switch from Semrush mid-subscription?

Yes — most Semrush alternatives allow you to import keyword lists, projects, and tracked URLs without lock-in. SE Ranking and Ahrefs both have straightforward onboarding. We recommend running both tools in parallel for 2–4 weeks during a trial period before canceling your Semrush subscription, so you can compare the data quality for your specific sites.

Our Final Recommendation

The best Semrush alternative depends entirely on your primary use case and budget:

  • If you need the same breadth of data at a lower price, SE Ranking is the strongest like-for-like swap — particularly for agencies who need white-label reporting.
  • If backlink research is your priority, Ahrefs is the better tool full stop, and the price difference from Semrush Pro is minimal.
  • If you're a beginner or focused on local SEO, Moz Pro's 30-day trial is the lowest-risk way to evaluate a full-suite alternative.
  • If keyword research on a budget is your core need, Mangools at $29/month is hard to beat.
  • If you're a content writer or strategist, Surfer SEO is a better-fit tool than Semrush for on-page optimization — it's a fundamentally different, workflow-first approach to content SEO.
  • If you want to start for free, Ubersuggest removes the financial barrier completely for basic keyword research.

From my experience, many SEO practitioners end up using two tools: a full-suite platform for research and rank tracking, and either Surfer or Ahrefs Webmaster Tools for content optimization or backlink monitoring. Running combinations through their free trials first is the most reliable way to find your right stack.
