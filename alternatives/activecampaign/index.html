Best ActiveCampaign Alternatives in 2026 (Compared)

Six honest alternatives to ActiveCampaign — compared on price, automation depth, free plans, and who each tool actually serves best.

Last updated: September 2026 · 6 alternatives compared · Independently reviewed

What is ActiveCampaign?

ActiveCampaign is one of the most capable email marketing and CRM platforms available today. Its visual automation builder lets you construct multi-step sequences triggered by subscriber behavior, lead scores, deal stages, and dozens of other conditions — all without writing code. The built-in CRM keeps sales and marketing data in sync, so follow-up emails can fire based on real pipeline events, not just time delays. It covers email campaigns, SMS, site tracking, and attribution in a single, deeply connected system that scales from solo operators up to enterprise teams.

That power comes with trade-offs. Pricing scales steeply with list size: a 10,000-contact list on the Plus plan runs well over $100 per month, and moving to the Professional tier for features like predictive sending or attribution reporting can push costs to $200–$400 monthly. The platform also has a meaningful learning curve — getting the most from its conditional logic, custom objects, and CRM pipelines takes real setup time. For businesses that need a simpler send-and-segment workflow, or that want to keep costs down while their list is still growing, looking at alternatives is a sensible exercise.

Why Look for an ActiveCampaign Alternative?

The most common reason people explore alternatives is cost. ActiveCampaign's contact-based pricing means your bill climbs quickly as your list grows, even if your sending volume stays modest. A small business paying $39/month at 1,000 contacts can find itself paying five times that by the time it reaches 25,000 contacts — without any corresponding increase in revenue to justify it.

Beyond price, some users find they simply don't need everything ActiveCampaign offers. If your marketing is a monthly newsletter plus a welcome sequence, you're paying for a visual automation builder, a built-in CRM, predictive sending, and site tracking that you'll never open. Tools like MailerLite or Mailchimp deliver everything you actually need at a fraction of the cost. On the other end of the spectrum, some users want a specific capability ActiveCampaign doesn't prioritize — live webinars (GetResponse), AMP interactive emails (Mailmodo), or creator-focused monetization tools (Kit). In all these cases, a targeted alternative often serves you better than a general-purpose platform built around features you don't use.

The 6 Best ActiveCampaign Alternatives

Each option below was selected because it addresses a specific gap: price, simplicity, a unique feature set, or a particular audience. We've noted who each tool is genuinely best suited for so you can narrow down quickly.

1. Mailmodo

Best for interactive emails
★★★★★ 4.5/5

Mailmodo is the standout choice if you want to do something ActiveCampaign genuinely cannot: send AMP-powered interactive emails that let subscribers fill out forms, book appointments, answer surveys, or complete purchases directly inside the inbox — no redirect required. That alone can meaningfully lift engagement rates, because you're removing the friction of asking subscribers to click through to a landing page for simple actions. Mailmodo also includes a solid automation builder and detailed campaign analytics, so it works as a full email platform, not just an AMP novelty.

Key strength: AMP email technology — interactive experiences inside Gmail and other AMP-compatible inboxes.
Best for: SaaS companies, e-commerce brands, and marketers who want to improve conversion rates through in-email interactivity.
Starting price: From $99/month (14-day free trial available)

+ Unique AMP email capability · + Strong deliverability · − Premium pricing · − Fewer integrations than AC

2. Mailchimp

Best for ease of use
★★★★★ 4.4/5

Mailchimp is the most widely used email marketing platform in the world for a reason: it is genuinely easy to set up and use, even for someone who has never run an email campaign before. Its drag-and-drop editor, pre-built templates, and audience segmentation tools cover the needs of the vast majority of small businesses and independent marketers. The free plan — up to 500 contacts with basic campaign sending — means you can get started and grow your list before you spend anything. Automations like welcome sequences and abandoned cart flows are available on paid plans starting at $13/month.

Key strength: The industry's most polished onboarding experience and largest template library.
Best for: Small businesses, retailers, and marketers new to email who need to get campaigns running fast.
Starting price: Free (up to 500 contacts) / Essentials from $13/month

+ Free plan available · + Very beginner-friendly · − Gets expensive at scale · − Lighter automation than AC
Try Mailchimp

3. GetResponse

Best all-in-one value
★★★★★ 4.5/5

GetResponse is a compelling all-in-one alternative that bundles email marketing, marketing automation, landing page creation, and webinar hosting into a single subscription. That last point matters: if you're currently paying separately for an email tool and a webinar platform, GetResponse often costs less combined than those two tools together. Its automation builder supports conditional branching, lead scoring, and e-commerce triggers — covering the majority of workflows that draw people to ActiveCampaign in the first place. A free plan for up to 500 contacts makes it easy to evaluate before committing.

Key strength: Built-in webinar hosting and landing pages included at no extra cost.
Best for: Coaches, consultants, course creators, and growing businesses that want email, pages, and webinars on one platform.
Starting price: Free plan (500 contacts) / Email Marketing from ~$19/month

+ Webinars + landing pages included · + Strong automation · − Template editor feels dated · − Lighter CRM than AC
Try GetResponse

4. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)

Best for large contact lists
★★★★★ 4.5/5

Brevo (the platform formerly known as Sendinblue) takes a different approach to pricing: instead of charging by contact count, it charges by the number of emails you send each month. This makes it dramatically cheaper than ActiveCampaign for businesses that have large lists but send infrequently — a B2B company with 50,000 contacts sending one campaign per month, for example, pays a fraction of what it would on a contact-based plan. Brevo also includes SMS marketing, a built-in CRM, transactional email support, and a free plan that allows 300 emails per day with unlimited contacts stored.

Key strength: Send-volume pricing instead of contact-based pricing — far cheaper for large, low-frequency senders.
Best for: Businesses with large contact lists, B2B marketers, and teams that also need SMS alongside email.
Starting price: Free (300 emails/day, unlimited contacts) / Starter from $9/month

+ Priced per email, not contacts · + Email + SMS + CRM in one · − Basic template editor · − Lighter advanced automation
Try Brevo

5. Kit (formerly ConvertKit)

Best for creators
★★★★★ 4.5/5

Kit was built specifically for independent creators — bloggers, podcasters, course sellers, and newsletter writers — and that focus shows throughout the product. Its subscriber tagging system is more flexible than segment-based tools for managing an audience that grows across multiple content formats. The visual automation builder is clean and approachable without sacrificing the ability to build meaningful conditional sequences. Paid newsletter features, a free landing page builder, and monetization tools round out an ecosystem that ActiveCampaign simply doesn't address for this audience. A generous free plan covers up to 10,000 subscribers for basic broadcasting.

Key strength: Creator-first audience management, paid newsletters, and monetization built in from day one.
Best for: Bloggers, newsletter writers, podcasters, course creators, and anyone building an audience around content.
Starting price: Free (up to 10,000 subscribers) / Creator from $9/month

+ Excellent free plan (10k subs) · + Built-in paid newsletters · − Light on email templates · − No CRM
Try Kit

6. MailerLite

Best simplicity + value
★★★★★ 4.5/5

MailerLite consistently earns its place on best-value lists for good reason. Its free plan covers up to 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 emails per month — more than enough for early-stage businesses to run proper campaigns. Paid plans start at just $9/month and unlock automation workflows, A/B testing, custom HTML editors, and a website and landing page builder. The editor is among the cleanest in the industry: fast, intuitive, and consistent enough that new team members can produce on-brand emails without training. For small businesses and solopreneurs who found ActiveCampaign overwhelming or over-priced, MailerLite is the most frequent landing spot.

Key strength: Generous free plan, rock-bottom paid pricing, and a cleaner editor experience than most tools in this range.
Best for: Small businesses, bloggers, solopreneurs, and nonprofits who need reliable email without complexity or high cost.
Starting price: Free (up to 1,000 subscribers) / Growing Business from $9/month

+ Very generous free plan · + Clean intuitive editor · − Less automation depth than AC · − No built-in CRM
Try MailerLite

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free alternative to ActiveCampaign?

Yes — several strong free alternatives exist. MailerLite offers a free plan for up to 1,000 subscribers (12,000 emails per month). Brevo's free tier allows up to 300 emails per day with unlimited contacts. Mailchimp provides a free plan for up to 500 contacts. These free tiers are genuinely usable for early-stage lists, though advanced automation features are typically gated behind paid plans.

Which ActiveCampaign alternative is best for small businesses?

For most small businesses, MailerLite or Brevo will hit the sweet spot of affordability and features. MailerLite's paid plans start at around $9/month and include automation, landing pages, and a clean drag-and-drop editor. Brevo is particularly attractive if you have a large contact list but low send volume, since it prices by emails sent rather than contacts stored — a meaningful cost difference as your list grows.

Can GetResponse replace ActiveCampaign?

GetResponse can replace ActiveCampaign for most use cases. It offers comparable email automation, a built-in CRM, landing pages, and even webinar hosting — all under one roof. Where it falls short is in the depth of its conditional logic and lead-scoring compared to ActiveCampaign's more mature automation engine. If your workflows are moderately complex and you also want landing pages or webinars without extra subscriptions, GetResponse is a compelling, lower-cost replacement.

What is the cheapest ActiveCampaign alternative?

Brevo and MailerLite are the most affordable paid alternatives, both starting at around $9/month. Kit (formerly ConvertKit) also starts at $9/month for up to 300 subscribers on the paid plan. If budget is the primary concern, start with MailerLite's free plan (up to 1,000 subscribers) or Brevo's free tier (300 emails/day, unlimited contacts) and upgrade only when you outgrow them.
