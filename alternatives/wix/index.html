Best Wix Alternatives in 2026 (Compared)

Six honest alternatives to Wix — compared on price, design quality, scalability, e-commerce, and who each platform actually serves best.

Last updated: November 2026 · 6 alternatives compared · Independently reviewed

What is Wix?

Wix is one of the most widely used drag-and-drop website builders in the world, with over 230 million users across its platform. It allows anyone to design and publish a website without writing code, using a visual editor that places elements freely on the canvas. Wix covers a wide range of site types — small business websites, portfolios, blogs, online stores, booking pages, and event sites — through its template library and app market. It hosts everything itself, so there is no separate hosting setup required. For millions of people building their first website, Wix lowers the barrier to a live, professional-looking online presence to nearly zero.

That said, Wix has meaningful limitations that send users looking elsewhere. Pricing has increased in recent years — paid plans start around $17/month, with e-commerce features requiring a Business plan at $36/month or higher. The platform's freeform editor creates beautiful designs but makes sites difficult to migrate: everything is stored in Wix's proprietary format, so moving to another platform later means rebuilding from scratch. As sites grow in complexity, performance can lag. And for developers or technically advanced users, Wix's abstractions can feel constraining compared to platforms that give you direct control over HTML, CSS, and hosting infrastructure.

Why Look for a Wix Alternative?

The most common reasons people explore alternatives to Wix fall into a few patterns. Price is frequently cited: Wix's plans have risen over the past few years, and competitors like Hostinger Website Builder offer comparable drag-and-drop builders at significantly lower monthly costs. Scalability is another driver — small businesses that start on Wix often find that as their needs grow (more complex e-commerce, custom functionality, performance optimisation), Wix's constraints become friction. Migration difficulty is a related concern: unlike WordPress or Webflow, Wix does not export your site in a portable format, which means committing to Wix is a longer-term decision than it might appear. Design quality is the fourth driver: Squarespace and Webflow consistently produce cleaner, more design-consistent results for brands that care about visual presentation as a differentiator.

None of this makes Wix a bad choice — for many use cases it remains an excellent option. But these are the real trade-offs that send a meaningful number of users toward the alternatives below.

The 6 Best Wix Alternatives

Each platform below was selected because it addresses a specific gap: price, design quality, e-commerce depth, developer control, or content management. We've noted who each tool is genuinely best suited for.

1. Squarespace

Best for design-conscious brands
★★★★★ 4.6/5

Squarespace is the strongest direct alternative to Wix for brands that care about visual presentation. Its templates are consistently cited as the most design-polished in the website builder category — award-winning designers choose Squarespace for portfolios precisely because the output looks professionally crafted rather than template-built. The editor works within a structured grid system (unlike Wix's fully freeform canvas), which trades some flexibility for consistency: it is harder to create a visually chaotic page in Squarespace, which is often a feature rather than a limitation. Built-in e-commerce, SEO tools, analytics, and marketing features are included at no extra app cost. Squarespace pricing starts around $16/month (annual billing), positioning it at slight premium over Wix's entry plans but below its mid-tier.

Key strength: Templates and design output that look premium by default, without needing a designer.
Best for: Creatives, agencies, portfolio sites, boutique brands, and any business where visual identity is a competitive differentiator.
Starting price: ~$16/month (annual billing)

+ Best-in-class design templates · + E-commerce + marketing included · − Less layout flexibility than Wix · − Smaller app ecosystem

2. WordPress.com

Best for content-first sites
★★★★★ 4.4/5

WordPress.com is the hosted version of WordPress — combining the world's most popular CMS engine with managed hosting, so you get the content management power of WordPress without configuring servers or installing plugins manually. It is a fundamentally different product from Wix: where Wix optimises for visual drag-and-drop design, WordPress.com optimises for content structure, SEO, and extensibility. Blogs, news sites, knowledge bases, and documentation sites that need category architecture, custom post types, and deep SEO control are better served by WordPress.com than by any pure website builder. The free plan exists for basic publishing at a wordpress.com subdomain; the Starter plan from around $9/month connects a custom domain.

Key strength: World-class blogging and CMS capability, SEO-friendly structure, and a path to self-hosted WordPress.org if you ever need it.
Best for: Bloggers, news sites, content-driven businesses, and anyone who expects to build significant written content over time.
Starting price: Free (wordpress.com subdomain) / ~$9/month (custom domain)

+ Best-in-class CMS and blogging · + Free plan available · − Higher learning curve than Wix · − Advanced features require higher tiers

3. Webflow

Best for design + dev control
★★★★★ 4.5/5

Webflow occupies a different tier from Wix: it gives designers and developers visual control over CSS-level properties — flexbox, grid, animations, interactions — through a browser-based design tool that generates clean, exportable HTML and CSS. The learning curve is considerably steeper than Wix, but the ceiling is also dramatically higher. Webflow sites tend to outperform on Core Web Vitals, offer more sophisticated interactions and micro-animations, and are migrateable (you can export the code). Agency sites, SaaS marketing pages, and design studios that would otherwise need a developer to maintain and update the site are Webflow's core use case. Plans start around $14/month for basic sites, with e-commerce and CMS plans scaling up from there.

Key strength: Near-code design control in a visual environment — the output is closer to custom-built than template-built.
Best for: Designers, agencies, startups with marketing teams, and developers who want a faster alternative to hand-coding.
Starting price: Free (Webflow subdomain) / ~$14/month (custom domain, Basic plan)

+ CSS-level design control · + Exportable code · − Steep learning curve · − Not suited for non-technical users
Try Webflow

4. Hostinger Website Builder

Best budget alternative
★★★★★ 4.4/5

Hostinger Website Builder is the most direct budget alternative to Wix — offering a drag-and-drop builder with an AI-assisted setup at a starting price around $2.99/month on annual plans. That's a fraction of Wix's entry-level pricing for a builder that covers the core use cases: business sites, portfolios, online stores, and blogs. Hostinger bundles in free hosting, an SSL certificate, and a free domain with most plans, so the total cost of getting a site live is lower than any comparable tool. The builder includes an AI site generator that creates a starting layout from a short description of your business, which speeds up initial setup considerably. It's not as design-polished as Squarespace or as powerful as Webflow, but for straightforward business sites on a budget, it's hard to beat the price-to-functionality ratio.

Key strength: The most affordable drag-and-drop website builder with AI-assisted setup included.
Best for: Small businesses, freelancers, and startups that want a professional website at the lowest possible monthly cost.
Starting price: ~$2.99/month (annual billing)

+ Cheapest in category · + AI site generator included · − Fewer templates than Wix · − Smaller app ecosystem
Try Hostinger

5. Shopify

Best for e-commerce
★★★★★ 4.7/5

If your primary reason for building a website is to sell products, Shopify is the most purpose-built alternative. Wix includes e-commerce features, but Shopify's entire platform is designed around the commerce workflow: product management, inventory across locations, multi-currency selling, advanced shipping rules, checkout customisation, and an app ecosystem of over 8,000 integrations for loyalty programs, reviews, subscriptions, and more. Shopify's checkout is widely considered the highest-converting in the industry, which is a meaningful consideration for any store where conversion rate has a direct revenue impact. Plans start at around $29/month; the Basic plan covers the needs of most growing stores. Shopify's content management for non-product pages is functional but less powerful than WordPress, making it a trade-off if you also run significant editorial content alongside your store.

Key strength: The industry's most comprehensive e-commerce infrastructure, from checkout to fulfilment to multi-channel selling.
Best for: Online retailers, DTC brands, and anyone whose primary goal is selling products at scale.
Starting price: ~$29/month (Basic plan, annual billing)

+ Best-in-class e-commerce tools · + High-converting checkout · − Overkill if selling isn't the focus · − Transaction fees without Shopify Payments

6. Framer

Best for modern AI-powered design
★★★★★ 4.4/5

Framer has emerged as a favourite among startup and SaaS marketing teams for building high-performance, visually sophisticated landing pages and marketing sites faster than traditional tools allow. Its AI-powered site generator can produce a complete starting layout from a text prompt, and the editor gives designers Figma-like control over responsive layouts, animations, and component behaviour. Framer sites are fast by default — they output clean code and host on a global CDN. For brands that previously would have hired a developer to build a custom marketing site, Framer covers a significant portion of that workflow. The free plan publishes to a framer.app subdomain; paid plans from around $10/month connect a custom domain and add CMS and analytics features.

Key strength: AI-assisted design with Figma-level layout control and excellent site performance out of the box.
Best for: Startups, SaaS companies, and designers building high-performance marketing sites without custom development.
Starting price: Free (framer.app subdomain) / ~$10/month (custom domain)

+ AI site generator · + Excellent performance · − Smaller template library than Wix · − Limited e-commerce capability

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free alternative to Wix?

Yes. WordPress.com offers a free plan that publishes at a wordpress.com subdomain. Webflow has a free Starter tier for personal projects. Framer publishes to a framer.app subdomain for free. The common limitation is that connecting a custom domain requires upgrading to a paid plan, typically starting at $9–$14 per month depending on the tool. Wix itself also has a free plan, though it displays Wix branding and ads.

What is the cheapest Wix alternative?

Hostinger Website Builder is the most affordable option, with plans starting around $2.99 per month on annual billing — roughly 5–6× cheaper than Wix's entry-level paid plan. WordPress.com's Starter plan is next at around $9/month. Both include a custom domain connection and basic business site capability. Squarespace starts around $16/month but includes more built-in features than Wix at that price point.

Can WordPress replace Wix?

WordPress.com can replace Wix for most content-focused sites — blogs, portfolios, and informational business websites — and provides better SEO structure and CMS depth. The trade-off is a steeper learning curve: WordPress uses a block editor rather than Wix's freeform drag-and-drop canvas. For users who prioritise ease of visual design over content architecture, Squarespace or Hostinger Website Builder are more direct replacements. Self-hosted WordPress.org (a different, more powerful product) adds even more capability but requires separate hosting setup.

Which Wix alternative is best for online stores?

Shopify is the strongest e-commerce alternative, with purpose-built product management, inventory tracking, multi-currency support, and a best-in-class checkout. It's the right choice when selling products is the primary goal of the site. Squarespace Commerce is a solid option for smaller shops that need design quality alongside selling capability. Hostinger Website Builder includes e-commerce features at a lower price point, suitable for small catalogs with simpler requirements.
