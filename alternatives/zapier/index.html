Best Zapier Alternatives in 2026 (Compared)

Six honest Zapier alternatives compared on price, workflow flexibility, integration count, and ease of use — so you can find the right automation platform for your budget and skill level.

Why Look for a Zapier Alternative?

Zapier is the market leader in no-code workflow automation, connecting 7,000+ apps and offering a polished, beginner-friendly interface. However, it has two well-known friction points. First, pricing: Zapier's paid plans start at ~$29.99/month but costs scale steeply with task volume, and many growing businesses find they need 10,000–50,000 tasks per month to cover their workflows — tiers that push monthly spend into hundreds of dollars. Second, the per-task pricing model means every automation step counts toward your quota, making multi-step Zaps expensive to run at scale.

The alternatives below address both issues. Some offer unlimited tasks at a fixed price, some are open-source, and some focus on specific use cases where Zapier is overbuilt. Editorial judgments that one alternative suits a use case better are ours; pricing should be verified on each vendor's current pricing page.

The Best Zapier Alternatives in 2026

1. Make (formerly Integromat) — Best Overall Zapier Alternative

★★★★★ 4.7/5 · From $9/month

Make is the strongest Zapier alternative for teams that want deep automation power without paying Zapier's enterprise prices. Its visual scenario builder is arguably more flexible than Zapier — it displays data flow between modules clearly, making complex multi-step workflows easier to debug and optimize.

Strengths

  • ✓ More affordable than Zapier at equivalent volumes
  • ✓ Visual scenario builder is powerful and clear
  • ✓ Free plan: 1,000 operations/month
  • ✓ Handles complex, branching logic well

Limitations

  • ✗ Steeper learning curve than Zapier
  • ✗ Fewer native integrations (2,000+ vs Zapier's 7,000+)
  • ✗ Interface less intuitive for complete beginners

Pricing: Free (1,000 ops/month), paid plans from ~$9/month (10,000 ops) to $29/month (40,000 ops), billed annually. Verify on make.com.

Best for: Teams that have outgrown Zapier's pricing but want comparable no-code power. Make is the go-to pick for most users switching from Zapier for cost reasons.

2. n8n — Best Free/Open-Source Option

★★★★☆ 4.4/5 · Free (self-hosted) / From $20/month (cloud)

n8n is an open-source workflow automation tool that you can self-host for free with no task limits. For technical users and developers, it is the most powerful Zapier alternative available — it supports custom JavaScript execution mid-workflow, connecting to any API, and can process unlimited automations on your own infrastructure.

Strengths

  • ✓ Free to self-host with unlimited workflows
  • ✓ Supports JavaScript code execution
  • ✓ Connects to any API, not just pre-built integrations
  • ✓ Active open-source community

Limitations

  • ✗ Requires server setup for self-hosted free use
  • ✗ Less beginner-friendly than Zapier
  • ✗ Cloud hosted version has per-workflow pricing

Pricing: Free to self-host. Cloud plans from ~$20/month. Verify on n8n.io.

Best for: Developers and technical teams who want maximum flexibility and zero usage limits, and are comfortable managing their own infrastructure.

3. Pabbly Connect — Best for One-Time Payment Buyers

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 · Lifetime deal available

Pabbly Connect is notable for offering lifetime deal pricing — a one-time payment for unlimited workflows and tasks. For small businesses and creators who want automation without ongoing subscription costs, this model is attractive. The integration library is smaller than Zapier or Make, but covers the most popular business apps.

Strengths

  • ✓ Lifetime deal option — no recurring fees
  • ✓ Unlimited tasks on paid plans
  • ✓ Covers all major apps (Shopify, Gmail, Stripe, etc.)
  • ✓ Good for high-volume simple automations

Limitations

  • ✗ Smaller integration library than Zapier
  • ✗ Less polished UI than Make or Zapier
  • ✗ Advanced features less mature

Pricing: Lifetime deal and monthly plans available. Verify on pabbly.com.

Best for: Budget-conscious businesses running high-volume automations who want to escape the per-task pricing treadmill with a one-time investment.

4. Zapier — Still the Easiest Option

★★★★★ 4.7/5 · Free plan / From ~$29.99/month

Worth acknowledging: Zapier remains the benchmark for ease of use and integration breadth. If your automation needs are modest and the pricing fits your budget, the original is often still the best choice. Its 7,000+ integrations, polished editor, and reliability mean that for straightforward two-step automations, no alternative matches the experience.

Strengths

  • ✓ 7,000+ integrations — the widest catalog
  • ✓ Most beginner-friendly setup experience
  • ✓ Reliable uptime and fast support
  • ✓ AI-powered Zap creation

Limitations

  • ✗ Expensive at scale — per-task pricing adds up
  • ✗ Each step in a multi-step Zap counts as a task
  • ✗ Limited flexibility for complex custom logic

Pricing: Free (100 tasks/month), paid from ~$29.99/month. Verify on zapier.com.

5. Activepieces — Growing Open-Source Alternative

★★★★☆ 4.2/5 · Free (self-hosted) / Cloud plans

Activepieces is an open-source workflow automation platform that has grown rapidly as a n8n alternative with a more Zapier-like interface. It is easier to self-host than n8n and has a growing library of pre-built integrations. The cloud-hosted version provides a managed experience with no server setup required.

Strengths

  • ✓ Open-source and self-hostable for free
  • ✓ More beginner-friendly than n8n
  • ✓ Rapidly growing integration library

Limitations

  • ✗ Smaller ecosystem than Zapier or Make
  • ✗ Still maturing — fewer advanced features

Best for: Teams wanting an open-source Zapier alternative that is easier to get started with than n8n.

6. Microsoft Power Automate — Best for Microsoft 365 Users

★★★★☆ 4.3/5 · Included with Microsoft 365

Microsoft Power Automate is the natural choice for organizations already running Microsoft 365, SharePoint, or Dynamics. It is included in many Microsoft 365 business plans at no extra cost, and its depth of integration with Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, and Excel is unmatched by any third-party tool. For non-Microsoft stacks, Make or Zapier serve better.

Best for: Microsoft 365 organizations wanting to automate workflows across Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, and Excel without additional software spend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free alternative to Zapier?

Yes — several strong free alternatives exist. n8n is open-source and free to self-host with no task limits if you run your own server. Make offers a free plan with 1,000 operations per month. Pabbly Connect is a one-time purchase rather than a subscription, which is effectively free once you've paid the upfront fee.

Which Zapier alternative is best for small businesses?

For most small businesses, Make hits the sweet spot of affordability and flexibility. The free plan covers basic automation, and paid plans start much lower than Zapier's equivalent tiers. Make's visual scenario builder is powerful enough for complex multi-step workflows without requiring coding skills.

Can n8n replace Zapier?

n8n can replace Zapier for most automation tasks, with significant advantages in flexibility and cost once you're comfortable self-hosting. It supports all major integrations, has a visual workflow builder, and allows JavaScript code execution mid-workflow. The trade-off is that self-hosting requires more technical setup than Zapier's plug-and-play experience.

What is the cheapest Zapier alternative?

n8n is free to self-host with no usage limits. For cloud-hosted options, Pabbly Connect offers lifetime deals that make it one of the most cost-effective long-term options. Make's free plan at 1,000 operations per month is a solid starting point for low-volume automation needs.

Our Recommendation

For most businesses switching from Zapier for cost reasons, Make is the best starting point — it closely mirrors Zapier's no-code experience while offering lower entry pricing and a more powerful visual workflow builder. If you have technical resources and want unlimited automations without any monthly spend, n8n's self-hosted version is unbeatable on value. For high-volume simple automations on a fixed budget, Pabbly Connect's lifetime deal is worth evaluating seriously.
