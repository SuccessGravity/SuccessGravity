Why Look for a HubSpot Alternative?

HubSpot has earned its reputation as the gold standard for CRM and inbound marketing software. Its free CRM is genuinely useful — robust contact management, deal pipelines, email tracking, and a live chat widget, all at no cost. The problem surfaces when growing businesses turn to the paid hubs: Marketing Hub Professional starts at roughly $800/month (billed annually) for 2,000 contacts, and the price climbs steeply as your list grows. For a team spending $800–$3,200/month on marketing software, the question of whether a focused alternative could do the same job for $50–$200/month is not just reasonable — it's obligatory.

The other common driver is complexity. HubSpot is a platform built for growth at scale, which means it carries significant configuration overhead for teams that just want to send email sequences, manage a sales pipeline, or track leads from a website. The alternatives below range from pure-play sales CRMs to full marketing automation suites, and each represents a legitimate, well-supported option depending on what part of HubSpot's feature set matters most to your business. Pricing below reflects publicly listed rates at the time of writing — always confirm on the vendor's current pricing page before committing.

1. ActiveCampaign — Best Overall HubSpot Alternative for Marketing Automation

★★★★★ 4.6/5 · From ~$15/mo

ActiveCampaign combines a visual email automation builder with a built-in CRM, making it the closest functional parallel to HubSpot's Marketing + Sales Hub combination at a fraction of the price. Its automation editor — where you build multi-step workflows triggered by contact behavior, site visits, form fills, or custom events — is widely regarded as the most powerful in its price range. Lead scoring, dynamic content, split testing, and one-click CRM updates are all available from the Starter plan at around $15/month for 1,000 contacts. According to ActiveCampaign's own documentation, it integrates with over 900 third-party apps, covering most of the same connectivity HubSpot offers. The main trade-off relative to HubSpot is reporting depth: ActiveCampaign's dashboards are functional but less visually polished than HubSpot's attribution reporting, which may matter to marketing teams that run regular ROI reviews for leadership.

2. Pipedrive — Best for Sales-Led Teams Who Just Need a CRM

★★★★★ 4.4/5 · From ~$14/mo

Pipedrive is built by salespeople for salespeople, and that focus is evident in every screen. The pipeline view — a drag-and-drop Kanban board showing every deal at every stage — is cleaner and faster than HubSpot's sales CRM interface, and it requires almost no configuration to be useful on day one. According to Pipedrive's pricing page, the Essential plan starts at roughly $14 per user per month (billed annually) and includes unlimited contacts, deals, and pipelines. For teams whose primary need is managing a sales pipeline — tracking conversations, logging activities, and moving deals forward — Pipedrive delivers more value per dollar than HubSpot's Sales Hub Professional. Where Pipedrive trails HubSpot is in marketing automation: its email marketing features are functional but not the deep sequencing and behavior-triggered workflow engine that HubSpot and ActiveCampaign offer. Teams that live in the CRM and don't need sophisticated email automation will find Pipedrive a better fit than paying for HubSpot features they never use.

3. Zoho CRM — Best Free HubSpot Alternative with Full-Suite Potential

★★★★★ 4.3/5 · Free / From ~$14/mo

Zoho CRM offers one of the most generous free plans in the CRM market — up to 3 users, core contact and lead management, task tracking, and a deals pipeline, with no time limit. For very small teams or solo operators who just need to keep track of prospects and follow-ups without paying anything, Zoho's free tier is hard to beat. Zoho's paid Standard plan starts at roughly $14 per user per month and adds email marketing, social media integration, sales forecasting, and custom reports. The broader Zoho One suite ($37/user/month) bundles more than 40 business applications — CRM, email, helpdesk, accounting, project management, and more — which makes it a compelling HubSpot replacement for businesses willing to go all-in on the Zoho ecosystem. The interface is denser and less polished than HubSpot's, and the learning curve is steeper, but the price-to-feature ratio at the suite level is exceptional for budget-conscious teams.

4. monday.com — Best for Teams That Want CRM + Project Management Together

★★★★★ 4.5/5 · Free / From ~$9/seat/mo

monday.com occupies a distinctive niche: it's primarily a work management and project management platform that has built a capable CRM layer on top, rather than the other way around. For companies where the line between project delivery and client relationship management is blurry — agencies, consulting firms, creative teams, and professional services businesses — monday.com CRM lets you manage leads and deals in the same workspace where you run projects, assign tasks, and track deliverables. According to monday.com's pricing page, the free plan supports 2 seats with basic boards; the Basic plan starts at roughly $9 per seat per month (3-seat minimum) and the CRM-specific features require the Standard tier at around $12/seat/month. The email marketing and automation depth is lighter than ActiveCampaign or HubSpot — monday.com is best for teams that want pipeline visibility and activity tracking in a flexible work management tool, not a full outbound email marketing engine.

5. GetResponse — Best for Email Marketing + Webinars Without the HubSpot Price

★★★★★ 4.3/5 · Free / From ~$15/mo

GetResponse is a marketing platform that bundles email campaigns, automation workflows, landing pages, webinars, and basic e-commerce tools into a single subscription — covering much of what HubSpot's Marketing Hub Starter offers but starting at roughly $15 per month for 1,000 contacts (with a free plan for up to 500 contacts). Its webinar functionality is a notable differentiator: GetResponse includes live and on-demand webinar hosting on its Marketing Automation and higher plans, which HubSpot does not offer natively. For coaches, course creators, B2B companies running product demos, and businesses where webinars are a lead generation channel, this bundling delivers meaningful cost savings versus paying for HubSpot plus a separate webinar tool. The CRM functionality is lighter than HubSpot's — GetResponse tracks contacts and basic lifecycle stages but doesn't offer the same depth of deal pipeline management as Pipedrive or Zoho CRM. It suits marketing-led teams more than sales-led ones.

6. Freshsales — Best for Customer Support + CRM Combined

★★★★★ 4.3/5 · Free / From ~$15/mo

Freshsales (part of the Freshworks suite, which also includes Freshdesk for customer support) offers a free CRM tier with unlimited users — covering contacts, accounts, deals, and a mobile app — that makes it one of the most accessible free HubSpot alternatives available. The paid Growth plan starts at roughly $15 per user per month and adds AI-powered lead scoring, visual sales pipelines, email campaigns, and phone integration. One of Freshsales' structural advantages over HubSpot for service-forward businesses is the ease of pairing it with Freshdesk: for companies where the same customer may be both a prospect and a support ticket raiser, having CRM and helpdesk from the same vendor with native data sharing simplifies the stack considerably. HubSpot covers service desk with its Service Hub, but the Freshworks bundle is typically less expensive for comparable functionality. Freshsales lacks HubSpot's breadth of marketing automation at the entry tier but closes the gap on Growth and Pro plans.

Which HubSpot Alternative Should You Choose?

  • Best overall alternative: ActiveCampaign — deep email automation + CRM at $15/mo covers 80% of what HubSpot Marketing + Sales Hub does for most SMBs.
  • Best for pure sales pipeline management: Pipedrive — clean, fast CRM pipeline with minimal setup. Your sales team will use it from day one.
  • Best free alternative: Zoho CRM (free for 3 users) or Freshsales (free for unlimited users) — both are fully usable long-term free tiers, not crippled trial versions.
  • Best for teams that already use project management tools: monday.com — especially if you want CRM visibility inside the same workspace where you run projects.
  • Best for webinar-driven lead gen: GetResponse — replaces HubSpot Marketing + a separate webinar tool in one subscription.
  • Best for customer service + CRM together: Freshsales + Freshdesk — native pairing of CRM and helpdesk from the same vendor.

A note on keeping HubSpot: if your team has already built significant automation logic, contact history, or reporting inside HubSpot, don't migrate without a real cost-benefit analysis. The free CRM tier is genuinely valuable, and keeping it alongside a cheaper email marketing tool — such as ActiveCampaign or GetResponse — is a common hybrid approach that avoids a full migration while reducing the Marketing Hub bill.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free HubSpot alternative?

Yes. Zoho CRM offers a free plan for up to 3 users with core contact management, pipelines, and task management — no credit card required. HubSpot's own free CRM is generous and worth keeping if you're only using the CRM layer. GetResponse has a free plan for up to 500 contacts. Freshsales (by Freshworks) also offers a free CRM plan. The key is to identify which HubSpot Hub you actually pay for — often the CRM itself is free, and it's the Marketing Hub Professional tier (~$800/mo) that triggers the search for alternatives.

What is the best HubSpot alternative for small business?

ActiveCampaign is the strongest small-business HubSpot alternative for teams that need email automation plus CRM together. It starts at ~$15/mo, includes a visual automation builder, a built-in CRM, and lead scoring — covering the core of what HubSpot's Marketing and Sales Hubs offer at a fraction of the combined price. Pipedrive is a better fit if your primary need is CRM pipeline management rather than email marketing.

Can I migrate from HubSpot to another platform?

Yes, but plan carefully. HubSpot exports contacts, companies, deals, and email history as CSV files. Most alternatives — ActiveCampaign, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Freshsales — accept CSV imports and have dedicated HubSpot migration guides. The main friction points are custom properties, email template formatting, and automation workflows: these typically need to be rebuilt manually in the new platform. If you have complex automation sequences, budget 1–2 days of setup time. ActiveCampaign and Zoho CRM both have documented HubSpot migration paths.

Which HubSpot alternative has the best email marketing?

ActiveCampaign is the strongest email marketing HubSpot alternative, particularly for automation depth. Its visual automation builder rivals HubSpot's workflow editor at a much lower entry price. GetResponse is a close second — especially if you also need webinars, landing pages, and e-commerce integrations bundled with email. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is worth considering if you send high volumes and want to pay per email sent rather than per contact stored, which can dramatically reduce costs for large lists with moderate send frequency.
